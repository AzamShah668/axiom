## opening
1964. America ke New Jersey mein, do scientists ek giant radio antenna ke andar se, kabootaron ki gandagi saaf kar rahe the. Arno Penzias aur Robert Wilson. Unke antenna mein ek ajeeb si sarsarahat thi, jo jaati hi nahi thi. Din ho ya raat, antenna kisi bhi taraf mude, awaaz wahi rehti. Unhe laga, yeh sab kabootaron aur unke ghonslon ki wajah se hai. Kabootar hataaye gaye. Antenna chamka diya gaya. Sarsarahat phir bhi wahi. Woh sarsarahat koi kharaabi nahi thi. Woh brahmaand ki sabse purani roshni thi... Big Bang ki goonj. Isi goonj ne saabit kiya, ki brahmaand ki ek shuruaat thi. Aaj hum jaante hain, woh shuruaat 13.8 arab saal pehle hui. Aur scientists ke hisaab se, uske pehle hi pal mein, brahmaand 10 ke aage 26 zero guna bada ho gaya. Is khoj ne aage chal kar Nobel Prize jeeta. Lekin insaan ke saamne sabse bada sawaal bhi khada kar diya. Brahmaand shuru hone se pehle, aakhir kya tha?

## ch1
**Card:** `THE UNIVERSE HAS A BIRTHDAY` (chapter card, no number)

Brahmaand ka bhi ek janamdin hai. Aur scientists use ekdum sateek jaante hain. 2009 mein, Europe ki space agency ne ek satellite bheja, Planck. Chaar saal tak usne poore aasmaan mein wahi roshni naapi, jo New Jersey ke antenna ne pakdi thi. Us roshni mein chhupe halke se nishaan, brahmaand ki umar batate hain. 2018 mein, Planck ka aakhri hisaab aaya. 13.8 arab saal. Aur galti ki gunjaaish, sirf 2 crore saal. Matlab jab hamari Earth bani, lagbhag 4.6 arab saal pehle, tab tak brahmaand pehle hi 9 arab saal purana ho chuka tha. Lekin brahmaand ki koi shuruaat thi, yeh baat sabse pehle kisi telescope ne nahi, ek padri ne kahi thi.

## ch2
**Card:** `A PRIEST SAID IT FIRST` (chapter card, no number)

Belgium ke Father Georges Lemaître ek Catholic padri the. Aur saath mein ek physicist bhi. 1927 mein, unhone Einstein ki equations se dikhaya, ki brahmaand ek jagah tika hua nahi hai. Woh phail raha hai. Usi saal Brussels mein, Einstein ne Lemaître se kaha, aapke calculations sahi hain, lekin aapki physics bekaar hai. Do saal baad, 1929 mein, America ke Edwin Hubble ne dekha, ki door ki galaxies hum se door bhaag rahi hain. Aur jo jitni door hai, woh utni tez. 1931 mein Lemaître ne agla kadam uthaya. Agar sab kuch aaj phail raha hai, to kabhi na kabhi, sab kuch ek saath, ek jagah raha hoga. Unhone use naam diya, primeval atom. Brahmaand ka pehla anda. Is idea ko ek naam ki zaroorat thi. Aur woh naam diya, is theory ke sabse bade virodhi ne.

## ch3
**Card:** `A NAME FROM ITS BIGGEST CRITIC` (chapter card, no number)

28 March 1949. BBC radio par, British astronomer Fred Hoyle bol rahe the. Hoyle maante the, ki brahmaand ki na koi shuruaat hai, na koi ant. Woh hamesha se aisa hi hai. Lemaître wali theory samjhaate hue, unhone ek shabd bola, Big Bang. Ek dhamaka. Hoyle ne kabhi is theory ko nahi maana. Lekin unka diya naam, hamesha ke liye chipak gaya. Aaj duniya ka har bachcha brahmaand ki shuruaat ko, ussi naam se jaanta hai, jo ek virodhi ke muh se nikla tha. Aur Hoyle ko galat saabit kiya, ussi sarsarahat ne, jo aaj bhi hamare chaaron taraf hai.

## ch4
**Card:** `THE OLDEST LIGHT IS ALL AROUND US` (chapter card, no number)

Big Bang ke baad, brahmaand itna garam aur ghana tha, ki roshni ek kadam bhi aage nahi badh paati thi. Lagbhag 3 lakh 80 hazaar saal baad, brahmaand itna thanda hua, ki roshni pehli baar azaad hui. Wahi roshni, 13.8 arab saal se safar kar rahi hai. Safar mein woh itni khinch gayi, ki ab aankh se dikhne wali roshni nahi rahi. Ab woh microwaves hai, aur uska taapmaan sirf minus 270 degree Celsius. 1978 mein, Penzias aur Wilson ko is khoj ke liye Nobel Prize mila. Aur purane TV ki woh kaali safed jhilmil, jo channel na milne par aati thi, uska lagbhag 1 percent hissa, yahi roshni thi. Big Bang ki goonj, ghar ke TV par. Lekin is roshni mein ek pahaili bhi chhupi thi. Ek aisi pahaili, jisne scientists ko saalon tak pareshaan rakha.

## ch5
**Card:** `IT GREW 10²⁶ TIMES IN A FLASH` (chapter card, no number)

Brahmaand har taraf ek jaisa dikhta hai. Uske door door ke hisse, kabhi ek doosre se mile hi nahi, phir bhi bilkul ek jaise. Yahi thi pahaili. 7 December 1979 ki raat, America ke ek young physicist, Alan Guth, ne apni notebook mein likha, SPECTACULAR REALIZATION. Shaandaar khayal. Unka idea tha, ki shuruaat ke ek second ke bahut hi chhote hisse mein, space khud, roshni ki raftaar se bhi tez phaila. 10 ke aage 26 zero guna. Jitna brahmaand hum aaj dekh sakte hain, woh tab ek proton se bhi chhota tha. Aur palak jhapakne se bhi kam waqt mein, woh ek bade santre jitna ho gaya. Is phailaav ko naam mila, inflation. Inflation ne bata diya, ki brahmaand phaila kaise. Lekin jaise hi scientists is ghadi ko aur peeche ghumate hain, poori science hi toot jaati hai.

## ch6
**Card:** `WHERE PHYSICS BREAKS` (chapter card, no number)

Physics ki do sabse badi theories hain. Einstein ki gravity, jo badi cheezon ko samjhati hai, galaxies aur black holes ko. Aur quantum physics, jo sabse chhoti cheezon ko, atom aur usse bhi chhote kanon ko. Aam taur par dono apni apni jagah kaam karti hain. Lekin Big Bang ke pehle pal mein, sab kuch bahut chhota bhi tha, aur bahut bhaari bhi. Wahan dono theories ek doosre se lad padti hain. Yahan ek deewar hai. Ek second ko itne tukdon mein kaato, ki 1 ke aage 43 zero lag jaayein, Big Bang ke baad ka woh sabse pehla tukda. Isse Planck time kehte hain. Is deewar ke us paar kya hai, aaj tak koi equation nahi bata paayi. Lekin scientists ne us deewar tak pahunchne ki koshish nahi chhodi.

## ch7
**Card:** `THE HUNT FOR CLUES` (chapter card, no number)

17 March 2014 ko, Antarctica ke BICEP2 telescope ki team ne elaan kiya, ki unhe inflation ke seedhe nishaan mil gaye. Duniya bhar mein khushi mana li gayi. Lekin kuch mahino baad pata chala, ki woh nishaan, hamari hi galaxy ki dhool ne banaye the. Phir aaya James Webb Space Telescope. 2025 mein, Webb ne ab tak ki sabse door ki galaxy dekhi, jiski roshni Big Bang ke sirf 28 crore saal baad chali thi. Webb shuruaat ke jitna paas pahunch raha hai, utna hi saaf ho raha hai, ki deewar ke us paar jaane ke liye, sirf telescope kaafi nahi. Ab chahiye naye idea. Aur sabse hairaan karne wala idea diya, Stephen Hawking ne.

## ch8
**Card:** `MAYBE THERE WAS NO "BEFORE"` (chapter card, no number)

Ho sakta hai, ki Big Bang se pehle kuch tha hi nahi... Waqt bhi nahi. 1983 mein, Stephen Hawking aur James Hartle ne ek idea diya. Shuruaat ke paas, waqt bilkul waisa nahi chalta, jaisa hum jaante hain. Waqt bhi, lambaai chaudaai jaisa hi ban jaata hai. Matlab brahmaand ki shuruaat ka koi kinaara hi nahi. Woh ek gend ki tarah, bas gol ho kar band ho jaata hai. 2018 mein, apni maut se kuch hafte pehle, Hawking ne ise ek misaal se samjhaya. Big Bang se pehle kya tha, yeh poochhna aisa hi hai, jaise poochhna ki South Pole ke aur dakshin mein kya hai. Wahan kuch nahi hai. Kyunki South Pole khud, aakhri bindu hai. Agar Hawking sahi the, to "pehle" shabd ka hi koi matlab nahi. Lekin har scientist Hawking se sehmat nahi. Kuch kehte hain, Big Bang se pehle ek poora brahmaand tha.

## ch9
**Card:** `MAYBE THE UNIVERSE BOUNCED` (chapter card, no number)

Is idea ke hisaab se, hamare brahmaand se pehle ek aur brahmaand tha. Woh sikudta gaya, sikudta gaya, aur phir ek gend ki tarah wapas uchhal gaya. Use kehte hain... Big Bounce. Nobel Prize jeetne wale British scientist, Roger Penrose, isse bhi aage jaate hain. Unke hisaab se brahmaand ek ke baad ek, kabhi na khatam hone wale chakkaron mein chalta hai. 2018 mein, Penrose ki team ne daava kiya, ki Big Bang ki goonj mein, pichhle brahmaand ke nishaan dikhte hain. Zyaadatar scientists is daave ko nahi maante. Unke hisaab se woh sirf shor hai. Lekin agar Penrose sahi hain, to Big Bang kahaani ki shuruaat nahi, bas ek naya chapter tha. Aur ek idea aisa bhi hai, jo inse bhi aage jaata hai. Ki hamara Big Bang... akela tha hi nahi.

## ch10
**Card:** `ONE BUBBLE AMONG MANY` (chapter card, no number)

Inflation ki theory ka ek ajeeb nateeja hai. Kuch scientists ke hisaab se, woh tez phailaav kabhi har jagah ek saath ruka hi nahi. Jahan ruka, wahan ek bubble bana, ek brahmaand. Aur baaki jagah, phailaav chalta raha. Bilkul waise hi, jaise ubalte paani mein jagah jagah bulbule uthte hain. Is idea ko kehte hain eternal inflation. Isse ek ke baad ek, anginat bubbles bante rehte hain. Har bubble, ek alag brahmaand. Har ek ka apna Big Bang. Hamara brahmaand, shayad unmein se bas ek bubble hai... multiverse ka ek chhota sa hissa. Scientists ne Big Bang ki goonj mein, doosre bubbles se takkar ke nishaan bhi dhoondhe hain. Abhi tak, koi pakka nishaan nahi mila.

## ending
Jawaab ka intezaar shayad ab bahut lamba nahi. Japan ka LiteBIRD satellite, lagbhag 2032 mein jaane ki taiyaari mein hai. Uska kaam hai, us goonj mein inflation ke asli nishaan dhoondhna. Shayad wahi batayega, ki shuruaat kaise hui. Tab tak, sawaal khula hai. Hawking sahi the, ya Penrose? Waqt ki sach mein ek shuruaat thi, brahmaand ek kabhi na khatam hone wala chakkar hai, ya hamara brahmaand anginat bubbles mein se bas ek hai? Science abhi tak faisla nahi kar paayi. 1964 mein, Penzias aur Wilson ne jis sarsarahat ko kharaabi samjha tha, wahi aaj is sabse badi pahaili ka sabse bada suraag hai. Brahmaand kahan se aaya. Aur us se pehle kya tha. Iska jawaab, abhi bhi us sabse purani roshni ke peeche chhupa hai.
