OPENING
Ya to insaan brahmand mein akela hai, ya nahi. Aur dono hi baatein, utni hi darawni hain. Hamari galaxy, Milky Way, mein lagbhag 200 billion taare hain. Unme se zyaadatar taaron ke paas planets hain. Aur scientists ke hisaab se, arabon planets size mein Earth jaise hain. Woh apne taare se itni doori par hain, ki wahan paani beh sakta hai. Yeh galaxy 13 billion saal se bhi zyaada purani hai. Agar kahin bhi koi samajhdaar civilization bani hoti, to itne lambe time mein, woh poori galaxy mein phail chuki hoti. Uske signals, uske spacecraft, ya woh khud. Lekin antariksh se ab tak sirf sannata mila hai. Koi signal nahi. Koi mehmaan nahi. Koi nishaan nahi. Scientists ke saamne yahi sabse bada sawaal hai. Agar brahmand mein zindagi ke itne mauke hain, to sab hain kahan? Yeh sawaal sabse pehle kisi lab mein nahi, ek lunch ki table par poocha gaya tha.

CHAPTER: WHERE IS EVERYBODY?
1950 ki garmiyon mein, America ki Los Alamos lab se, chaar scientists lunch ke liye nikle. Unme se ek the Enrico Fermi. Nobel Prize jeet chuke, aur duniya ka pehla nuclear reactor banane wale scientist. Raaste mein baat chal rahi thi ek magazine cartoon ki. Usme aliens, New York ke kachre ke dabbe chura kar le ja rahe the. Sab hanse, aur baat aage badh gayi. Phir khaane ke beech, Fermi ne achanak poocha, "But where is everybody?" Table par baithe sab log samajh gaye, ki woh aliens ki baat kar rahe hain. Un scientists ne baad mein bataya, ki Fermi ne turant hisaab bhi lagaya tha. Galaxy bahut purani hai. Koi civilization, dheeme spacecraft se bhi, ek taare se agle taare tak jaaye, aur wahan basti basaye. Phir wahan se aage badhe, to kuch million saal mein poori galaxy bhar jaati. Galaxy ki umar ke saamne, yeh time bahut chhota hai. Yeh bilkul aisa hai, jaise ek bahut bada stadium, jiske gate karodon saal se khule hon, lekin andar har seat khaali mile. Aage chal kar is sawaal ko naam mila, Fermi Paradox. Paradox, yaani do baatein, jo dono sahi lagti hain, lekin ek saath sahi nahi ho sakti. Yeh koi proof nahi, ki aliens hone hi chahiye. Yeh expectation aur observation ke beech ka ek tension hai. Zindagi ke mauke bahut hain. Aur zindagi ka koi nishaan nahi. Fermi ne sirf sawaal poocha tha. Gyaarah saal baad, ek young astronomer ne is sawaal ko numbers mein todne ki koshish ki.

CHAPTER: THE EQUATION
Woh astronomer the Frank Drake. 1960 mein, unhone America ke Green Bank mein, ek bade radio telescope ka rukh, do paas ke taaron ki taraf kiya. Idea seedha tha. Agar wahan koi civilization radio signal bhej rahi ho, to yeh telescope use pakad sakta tha. Kai hafton tak, roz ghanton sunne ke baad, mila sirf bekaar shor. Ek baar ek tez signal aaya bhi, lekin woh Earth se hi aaya tha. Agle saal, Drake ne Green Bank mein ek chhoti si meeting bulaayi. Meeting se pehle, unhone board par ek equation likhi. Yeh equation aliens ka pata nahi deti thi. Yeh sawaalon ki ek list thi, jinke jawaab milne par, ginti nikal sakti thi. Pehla hissa, galaxy mein har saal kitne taare bante hain. Phir unme se kitno ke paas planets hain. Kitne planets par zindagi shuru hoti hai. Kitni zindagi samajhdaar banti hai. Kitni civilizations signal bhejna seekhti hain. Aur aakhri hissa, aisi civilization kitne saal tak tikti hai. Yeh waise hi hai, jaise kisi shehar mein andaaza lagaana, ki raat ke teen baje kitne log jaag rahe hain. Shehar ki aabaadi, unme se kitne raat ko kaam karte hain, aur woh kitni der jaagte hain. Har hissa jod do, to ginti mil jaati hai. 1961 mein, inme se zyaadatar hisson ka jawaab kisi ke paas nahi tha. Drake ka apna andaaza tha, ki hamari galaxy mein lagbhag 10,000 civilizations ho sakti hain. Lekin woh hamesha kehte rahe, ki sab kuch aakhri hisse par tika hai. Equation ne bata diya, ki dhoondhna kya hai. Ab zaroorat thi ek signal ki. Aur 1977 mein, ek raat, aisa hi ek signal aaya.

CHAPTER: THE WOW! SIGNAL
August 1977. America ke Ohio mein, Big Ear naam ka radio telescope, aasmaan ko sun raha tha. Use koi chalaata nahi tha. Woh jo sunta, ek computer use kaagaz par numbers aur akshar mein chhaap deta. Kuch din baad, ek volunteer astronomer, Jerry Ehman, ghar par baith kar yeh kaagaz padh rahe the. Ek jagah unki nazar ruk gayi. Wahan numbers ki jagah akshar the. Yeh kisi bhasha ke akshar nahi the, balki signal ki strength thi, jo achanak bahut badh gayi thi. Ehman ne us par red pen se gola banaya, aur bagal mein likha, "Wow!" Is signal ki pehli khaasiyat thi uski frequency. Antariksh mein taare aur gas bhi radio waves chhodte hain, lekin woh bahut saari frequencies par phaili hoti hain, jaise radio ka shor, har station par. Yeh signal sirf ek patli si frequency par tha. Aur woh frequency thi 1420 megahertz, jis par hydrogen radio waves chhodta hai. Hydrogen brahmand mein sabse zyaada milne wali cheez hai, isliye scientists maante the, ki koi civilization signal bhejegi, to isi frequency par. Doosri khaasiyat thi uska time. Signal theek 72 second tak chala. Yeh time aliens ne nahi chuna tha. Earth ke ghoomne se, Big Ear ki nazar aasmaan par aage khisakti rehti thi, aur koi bhi point uske saamne se itne hi time mein guzar jaata tha. Matlab signal aasmaan se aaya tha, Earth se nahi. Jaise bekaar shor se bhare radio par, achanak ek saaf sur baj uthe. Lekin woh sur, dobara kabhi nahi baja. Scientists ne aasmaan ke us hisse ko, alag alag telescopes se, dasiyon baar suna. Kuch nahi mila. 2024 mein, kuch scientists ne ek alag wajah sujhaayi. Shayad wahan hydrogen gas ka ek thanda badal tha, jise kisi door ke taare ki achanak tez roshni ne, pal bhar ke liye chamka diya. Yeh baat bhi abhi pakki nahi hai. Wow signal kabhi lauta nahi. Aur jitna zyaada insaan ne suna, sannata utna hi gehra hota gaya. Tab kuch scientists ne sawaal ko palat diya. Shayad galaxy mein koi aisi deewar hai, jise paar karna lagbhag naamumkin hai.

CHAPTER: THE GREAT FILTER
1996 mein, economist Robin Hanson ne Drake ki equation ko, ek alag nazar se dekha. Ek bejaan patthar se le kar, signal bhejne wali civilization tak, raasta bahut lamba hai. Pehle zindagi shuru honi chahiye. Phir simple cells se complex cells banne chahiye. Phir dimaag, phir bhasha, phir technology. Hanson ne kaha, ki is raaste par kahin na kahin, ek kadam itna mushkil hai, ki lagbhag koi use paar nahi kar paata. Unhone use naam diya, The Great Filter. Jaise ek race, jisme arabon log daudte hain, lekin track par kahin ek oonchi deewar hai, aur finish line tak lagbhag koi nahi pahunchta. Asli sawaal yeh hai, ki yeh mushkil kadam kahan hai. Agar yeh peeche hai, to insaan khushkismat hai. Earth par simple cells ko complex cells banne mein, lagbhag 2 billion saal lage, aur shayad aisa sirf ek baar hua. Ho sakta hai, wahi sabse mushkil kadam ho. Lekin agar yeh kadam abhi aage hai, to har civilization, technology seekhne ke baad, khud ko khatam kar leti hai. Nuclear hathiyaar, ya koi aisi galti, jise sudhaara na ja sake. Isiliye philosopher Nick Bostrom ne likha tha, ki Mars par zindagi ka chhota sa fossil bhi mile, to woh buri khabar hogi. Kyunki uska matlab hoga, ki zindagi shuru hona aasaan hai, aur asli mushkil shayad aage hai. Yahi Drake ki equation ka aakhri hissa hai, ek civilization kitne saal tikti hai. Lekin kuch logon ka maanna hai, ki sannate ki wajah kuch aur hai. Shayad woh wahan hain, aur jaan boojh kar chup hain.

CHAPTER: THE ZOO AND THE DARK FOREST
1973 mein, MIT ke ek scientist, John Ball, ne ek idea diya, jise naam mila Zoo Hypothesis. Unke hisaab se, aliens Earth ko dekh rahe hain, lekin door se, bina chhede. Jaise kisi jungle ke protected hisse mein jaanwaron ko dekha jaata hai, lekin unhe chhua tak nahi jaata. Doosra idea isse kahin zyaada andhera hai. Yeh kisi scientist ne nahi, Chinese science fiction writer Liu Cixin ne, 2008 ki ek novel mein diya. Naam hai, The Dark Forest. Unke hisaab se, brahmand ek andhera jungle hai, jisme har civilization ek shikaari hai. Kisi ko nahi pata, ki doosra dost hai ya dushman. Isliye jo bhi aawaaz karta hai, woh nishaana ban jaata hai. Aur jo zinda rehna chahta hai, woh chup rehta hai. Yeh sirf ek kahaani hai, iska koi saboot nahi. Lekin insaan pehle hi aawaaz kar chuka hai. 1974 mein, Puerto Rico ke Arecibo telescope se, taaron ke ek jhund ki taraf, ek radio message bheja gaya. Woh wahan lagbhag 25,000 saal mein pahunchega. Aur 2010 mein, Stephen Hawking ne chetaawani di thi. Aliens se mulaaqaat shayad waisi ho, jaisi Columbus ki America ke purane logon se hui thi. Un logon ke liye, woh mulaaqaat achhi nahi rahi thi. Phir bhi, kai scientists ke hisaab se, sabse seedha jawaab kuch aur hai. Insaan ne abhi tak theek se dhoondha hi nahi.

CHAPTER: HAVE WE EVEN LOOKED?
2018 mein, astronomer Jason Wright aur unki team ne ek hisaab lagaya. Ab tak kitna aasmaan, kitni frequencies par, kitni gehraai tak suna gaya hai. Jawaab chaunkaane wala tha. Unki misaal mein, galaxy Earth ke saare samandar jitni hai. Aur insaan ne ab tak usme se, sirf ek bade bathtub jitna paani jaancha hai. Ek bathtub mein machhli na mile, to koi nahi kehta, ki samandar khaali hai. Isiliye 2015 mein, Stephen Hawking ke saath, Breakthrough Listen naam ka project shuru hua. Iska kaam hai, 10 lakh paas ke taaron ko dhyaan se sunna. Aur ab sirf sunna hi nahi, dekhna bhi mumkin hai. 1995 mein, pehli baar, hamare Suraj jaise kisi taare ke paas ek planet mila. Aaj yeh ginti 6,000 se upar hai. James Webb Space Telescope, kuch door ke planets ki hawa tak jaanch sakta hai. 2025 mein, Cambridge ki ek team ne ek planet ke baare mein daava kiya, jo 124 light year door hai. Uski hawa mein, ek aisi gas ke nishaan hain, jo Earth par sirf zindagi banati hai. Lekin zyaadatar scientists is daave ko nahi maante. Baad ki jaanch mein, saboot kamzor nikla. Phir bhi, insaan ke paas pehli baar, kisi aur duniya ki hawa jaanchne wale telescope hain. Lekin agar itna dhoondhne ke baad bhi kuch na mila, to ek aakhri jawaab bachta hai. Sabse seedha, aur sabse gehra.

ENDING
2000 mein, do scientists, Peter Ward aur Donald Brownlee, ne ek kitaab likhi, Rare Earth. Unke hisaab se, simple zindagi shayad aam ho, lekin jaanwar aur insaan jaisi zindagi ke liye, bahut saare ittefaq chahiye. Ek bada Chaand, jo Earth ke jhukaav ko sambhaal kar rakhe. Jupiter jaisa ek bada planet, jo kai patthar Earth se door rakhe. Aur ek zameen, jo hilti rahe, aur apna roop badalti rahe. Har ittefaq ke saath, Earth jaisi duniya aur kam hoti jaati hai. Ho sakta hai, ki poori galaxy mein, taaron ki taraf dekhne wali aankhein, sirf yahin hon. Fermi ke us sawaal ko, aaj 75 saal se zyaada ho chuke hain. Kya brahmand mein sab Dark Forest ke darr se chup hain, kya koi filter sabko rok deta hai, ya insaan sach mein akela hai? Science abhi tak faisla nahi kar paayi. Los Alamos ki us lunch table par poocha gaya sawaal, aaj bhi jawaab ka intezaar kar raha hai.
