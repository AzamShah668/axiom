- **Third-person storytelling**, one continuous story from 1054 to 2025. It never addresses the viewer and never
  announces numbers; the countdown lives only on the chapter cards.
- **Story spine:**
  - In 1054, court astronomers in China see a "guest star". What it left behind, a city-sized star spinning
    30 times a second, took humanity 900 years to understand.
  - The countdown runs from the 1934 prediction to the 2025 discovery that the 2004 magnetar flare forged gold.
  - The ending returns to the guest star.
- **Open loops:**

  - #6 leaves the flare's afterglow unexplained (paid off in #1)
