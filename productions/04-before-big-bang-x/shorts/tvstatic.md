**Title**
About 1% of Old TV Static Was Light From the Big Bang #shorts

**Description**
```
When an old TV found no channel, the screen filled with black-and-white static. About 1 percent of it was the oldest light in the universe. In 1964, Arno Penzias and Robert Wilson heard a strange signal from every direction; it was light that has travelled for 13.8 billion years, now microwaves at minus 270 degrees Celsius. They won the Nobel Prize in 1978.
```
