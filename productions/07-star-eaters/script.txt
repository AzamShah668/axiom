OPENING
Ek taara, hamare Sun jaisa, lagbhag 10 arab saal tak jalta rehta hai. Lekin kabhi-kabhi, uska ant sirf kuch hi ghanton mein ho jaata hai. Woh khinchkar lakhon kilometre lambi, chamakti hui gas ki ek dhaar ban jaata hai. Aur phir hamesha ke liye andhere mein kho jaata hai.
Brahmand mein har taara apni umar poori karke nahi marta. Kuch taaron ka shikaar kiya jaata hai. Aur kuch ke shikaari hote hain, jaise ki black holes.

CHAPTER: THE FLASH OF 2011
28 March 2011. NASA ke Swift satellite ne achanak aasmaan mein ek behad tez chamak detect ki. Yeh chamak Draco constellation ki taraf se aa rahi thi, aur ismein gamma rays thi.
Shuruaat mein astronomers ko laga ki yeh ek normal gamma ray burst hai. Yaani kisi marte hue taare ka aakhri dhamaka, jo kuch seconds ya minutes mein khatam ho jaata hai.
Lekin is baar aisa nahi hua.
Yeh chamak band hi nahi hui. Pehle ghanton tak, phir dinon tak, aur aakhir mein hafton tak. Aasmaan ke usi ek hisse se lagataar X-rays aa rahi thi.
Yeh jagah Earth se lagbhag 380 crore light years door thi. Aur wahan jo ho raha tha, woh 100 arab Suns se bhi zyaada chamak raha tha.
California ke astronomer Joshua Bloom aur unki team ne Hubble aur Chandra telescopes ko usi direction mein point kiya.
Tab pata chala ki yeh chamak ek shaant galaxy ke bilkul centre se aa rahi thi. Wahan ek bada black hole maujood tha, jo hamari galaxy ke black hole se shayad do guna zyaada massive tha.
Woh black hole lambe samay se bilkul shaant tha. Lekin phir, hamare Sun jaisa ek taara bhatakte hue uske bahut kareeb pahunch gaya.

CHAPTER: TORN APART
Black hole kisi taare ko bina seedha apne andar nigle bhi tod sakta hai. Is idea ko 1975 mein astrophysicist Jack Hills ne explain kiya tha.
Har massive black hole ke aas-paas ek invisible boundary hoti hai, jise tidal radius kehte hain. Is boundary ke andar gravity ka asar kuch alag hi ho jaata hai.
Taare ka jo hissa black hole ke sabse kareeb hota hai, us par gravity ka pull door wale hisse ke muqaable kahin zyaada hota hai.
Yahi difference taare ko kheenchkar lamba kar deta hai.
Agar kisi insaan ke saath bhi aisa ho, toh uske pairon par lagne wala gravitational pull, sir ke muqaable kahin zyaada hoga. Aur dheere-dheere poora shareer khinchkar ek patli lakeer ban jaayega.
1988 mein Stephen Hawking ne apni kitaab A Brief History of Time mein is process ke liye ek mazedaar naam use kiya tha.
Spaghettification.
2011 mein us taare ke saath bhi bilkul yahi hua.
Taare ki apni gravity, black hole ke gravitational pull ke saamne haar gayi. Pehle woh apni gol shape se badalkar ek boond jaisa bana. Phir dheere-dheere ek lambi dhaar mein badal gaya.
Aur kuch hi ghanton mein, woh poori tarah bikhar gaya.
Lekin black hole ne poore taare ko ek saath nahi nigla.
Taare ki lagbhag aadhi gas, hazaaron kilometre per second ki speed se, wapas space mein nikal gayi. Baaki aadhi gas black hole ke chaaron taraf ghoomne lagi.
Yeh gas itni tezi se ghoom rahi thi ki aapas mein friction ki wajah se lakhon degrees tak garam ho gayi. Aur is tarah bani roshni ki ek chamakti hui disk, jise accretion disk kehte hain.
Isi dauraan, black hole ke magnetic fields ne kuch gas ko capture kiya aur use do seedhi dhaaron mein space ke andar bahut door tak launch kar diya.
In dhaaron ko jets kehte hain.
Inki speed light ki speed ka lagbhag 99.5 percent thi.
Aur sabse interesting baat yeh thi ki inmein se ek jet ka direction seedha Earth ki taraf tha.
Isi wajah se humein yeh event itna zyaada bright dikhai diya.
Aise torn apart stars pehle bhi observe kiye gaye the. Lekin yeh pehli baar tha jab is destruction se nikla hua jet seedha Earth ki taraf point kar raha tha.

CHAPTER: THE BLACK HOLE THAT BURPED
Lekin 2011 wala black hole, brahmand ke sabse bade predators ke saamne kuch bhi nahi tha.
Aur kuch black holes ki activities aaj bhi scientists ko hairaan kar deti hain.
October 2018.
Ek telescope survey ne lagbhag 66.5 crore light years door ek aur unusual flash detect kiya.
Ek aur black hole ne ek taare ko tod diya tha.
Kuch mahino mein woh chamak dheere-dheere shaant ho gayi, aur scientists apne doosre observations mein busy ho gaye.
Lekin June 2021 mein, lagbhag teen saal baad, Harvard ki astronomer Yvette Cendes aur unki team ne us jagah ko dobara observe kiya. Iske liye New Mexico ke Very Large Array radio telescope ki madad li gayi.
Aur jo unhone dekha, woh kaafi surprising tha.
Woh black hole ek baar phir active ho chuka tha.
Bina kisi aur taare ko nigle, woh light ki aadhi speed se gas space mein phenk raha tha.
Cendes ne is process ko ek funny example se explain kiya.
Unke mutaabik, yeh kuch aisa tha jaise koi insaan khaana khaane ke kai saal baad achanak ek dakaar le.
Scientists ne is event ko mazaak mein ek naam diya.
Jetty McJetface.
Lekin aaj tak scientists ko pakka nahi pata ki black hole ne itne saalon tak us gas ko kahan store karke rakha tha.

CHAPTER: THE GIANT THAT SWALLOWS WHOLE
Ab baat karte hain brahmand ke sabse bade predators mein se ek ki.
Iska naam hai TON 618.
1957 mein, Mexico ki Tonantzintla Observatory mein do astronomers the, Braulio Iriarte aur Enrique Chavira. Woh aasmaan mein blue light emit karne wale objects ki ek list bana rahe the.
Unki list mein number 618 par ek faint blue object tha.
Us time kisi ko nahi pata tha ki woh asal mein kya hai.
1970 mein, Italy ke Bologna mein maujood radio telescope observations se pata chala ki woh koi normal star nahi, balki ek quasar hai.
Quasar ek aisi jagah hoti hai jahan ek extremely massive black hole aas-paas ki gas ko lagataar consume kar raha hota hai. Aur us process mein itni energy release hoti hai ki woh poori galaxy se bhi zyaada bright ho sakta hai.
2004 mein astronomers ne TON 618 ka mass estimate kiya.
Kuch estimates ke mutaabik, iska mass hamare Sun se lagbhag 6,600 crore guna zyaada hai.
Ise ultramassive black hole kaha jaata hai.
Iska event horizon, yaani woh boundary jiske andar jaane ke baad light bhi wapas nahi aa sakti, lagbhag 2,600 astronomical units tak phaila hua hai.
Ek astronomical unit ka matlab hai Earth aur Sun ke beech ki distance.
Comparison ke liye, Neptune Sun se sirf 30 astronomical units door hai.
Agar TON 618 ko hamare Sun ki jagah rakh diya jaaye, toh poora Solar System uske andar sama jaayega.
Aur light ko iske ek kinaare se doosre kinaare tak pahunchne mein lagbhag do hafte lag jaayenge.
Iske aas-paas ghoomti hui gas, lagbhag 140 lakh crore Suns jitni roshni produce karti hai.
Lekin itne massive black holes ki ek ajeeb baat hai.
Yeh aam taur par taaron ko todte nahi hain.
Iski wajah yeh hai ki black hole jitna massive hota hai, uska event horizon utni hi tezi se bada hota jaata hai. Lekin tidal radius usi rate se nahi badhta.
Jab black hole ka mass Sun se lagbhag 10 crore guna zyaada ho jaata hai, toh tidal radius event horizon ke andar chala jaata hai.
Iska matlab hai ki agar hamare Sun jaisa koi taara TON 618 ke paas pahunch jaaye, toh woh pehle ki tarah stretch hokar tootega nahi.
Koi spectacular flash nahi hoga.
TON 618 use poora ka poora, bilkul khamoshi se nigal jaayega.
Brahmand ke sabse bade predators, kabhi-kabhi bilkul khamoshi se apna shikaar karte hain.

CHAPTER: THE HUNTER AT HOME
TON 618 Earth se 1,000 crore light years se bhi zyaada door hai.
Lekin ek massive black hole hamari apni galaxy ke centre mein bhi maujood hai.
1974 mein American astronomers Bruce Balick aur Robert Brown ne Green Bank ke radio telescopes istemaal kiye. Unhone Milky Way ke centre mein ek powerful radio signal detect kiya.
Yeh jagah Earth se lagbhag 26,000 light years door thi.
Baad mein is object ko naam diya gaya Sagittarius A star.
Agle tees saalon tak, Andrea Ghez aur Reinhard Genzel ki teams ne us jagah ke aas-paas ghoomte taaron ki movements observe ki. Iske liye Keck aur Very Large Telescope ki madad li gayi.
Unmein se ek taara hai, S2. Jab yeh us invisible object ke sabse kareeb se guzarta hai, toh uski speed lagbhag 7,700 kilometre per second tak pahunch jaati hai.
Yaani light ki speed ka lagbhag 2.5 percent.
In taaron ki movements ne confirm kar diya ki wahan ek black hole maujood hai, jiska mass hamare Sun se lagbhag 40 lakh guna zyaada hai.
Is discovery ke liye 2020 mein Andrea Ghez aur Reinhard Genzel ko Physics ka Nobel Prize diya gaya.
Aur May 2022 mein Event Horizon Telescope ne Sagittarius A star ki pehli image duniya ke saamne rakhi.
Aaj yeh black hole kaafi shaant hai.
Iske aas-paas khaane ke liye zyaada gas maujood nahi hai.
Lekin hamesha se aisa nahi tha.

CHAPTER: A FLASH 200 YEARS AGO
June 2023.
France ki Strasbourg Observatory ke astronomer Frederic Marin aur unki team ne NASA ke IXPE satellite se mile data ka analysis kiya.
Sagittarius A star ke aas-paas maujood gas ke kuch clouds X-rays mein chamak rahe the.
Jab scientists ne is roshni ka polarization measure kiya, toh unhe ek surprising baat pata chali.
Yeh roshni un gas clouds ki apni nahi thi.
Asal mein, yeh Sagittarius A star se nikli hui ek purani flash thi. Yeh gas clouds se takrakar reflect hui thi, aur ab Earth tak pahunch rahi thi.
Calculations ke mutaabik, lagbhag 200 saal pehle yeh black hole kuch samay ke liye active ho gaya tha.
Shayad usne apne paas maujood kisi gas cloud ko consume kar liya tha.
Lagbhag ek saal tak, uski brightness aaj ke muqaable kam se kam 10 lakh guna zyaada thi.
Aur phir, woh dobara shaant ho gaya.
Scientists ke estimates ke mutaabik, hamari galaxy mein aisa har 10,000 se 1 lakh saal mein ek baar hota hai. Koi bhatakta hua taara is black hole ke kareeb aakar toot sakta hai.

ENDING
Lekin is shaant black hole ka asli roop shayad tab saamne aayega, jab hamari galaxy ka saamna ek bahut badi cosmic collision se hoga.
Hamari neighbouring galaxy Andromeda lagbhag 110 kilometre per second ki speed se hamari taraf aa rahi hai.
Pehle scientists ka maanna tha ki lagbhag 400 crore saal baad, dono galaxies ka takrana almost certain hai.
Lekin 2025 ki ek study ke mutaabik, agle 1,000 crore saalon ke andar is collision ke hone ki possibility lagbhag 50 percent hai.
Agar yeh collision hoti hai, toh arabon taare aur gas ke massive clouds galaxy ke centre ki taraf move kar sakte hain.
Sagittarius A star aur Andromeda ka black hole, ek doosre ke around orbit karte hue, aakhirkaar merge ho sakte hain.
Aur tab shayad hamara yeh soya hua black hole poori tarah active ho jaaye.
Ek ke baad ek taare toot sakte hain, aur galaxy ke centre mein ek powerful quasar ban sakta hai.
2011 mein Swift satellite ne jo dekha tha, woh sirf kisi door galaxy ki kahaani nahi thi.
Woh shayad hamari apni galaxy ke possible future ki ek jhalak thi.
Aasmaan mein taare humein shaant dikhai dete hain.
Lekin us andhere ke peeche gravity ka ek aisa khel lagataar chal raha hai, jise hum abhi poori tarah samajh bhi nahi paaye hain.
Aur brahmand ke yeh shikaari...
Bas apne agle bhatakte hue taare ka intezaar kar rahe hain.
