OPENING
Ek taara, hamare Sun jaisa, lagbhag 10 arab saal tak jalta hai. Lekin kabhi kabhi, uska ant sirf kuch ghanton mein ho jaata hai. Woh kheench kar lakhon kilometre lambi, chamakti gas ki ek dhaar ban jaata hai. Aur phir hamesha ke liye andhere mein kho jaata hai. Brahmand mein har taara apni umar poori karke nahi marta. Kuch taaron ka shikaar hota hai. Aur shikaari hai, ek black hole.

CHAPTER: THE FLASH OF 2011
28 March 2011. NASA ke Swift satellite ne aasmaan mein achanak ek bahut tez chamak pakdi. Woh Draco constellation ki taraf se aa rahi thi, aur usme gamma rays thi. Pehle astronomers ko laga, ki yeh ek aam gamma ray burst hai. Kisi marte hue taare ka aakhri dhamaka, jo kuch second ya kuch minute mein shaant ho jaata hai. Lekin yeh chamak band nahi hui. Woh ghanton tak chali, phir dinon tak, phir hafton tak. Aasmaan ke usi ek hisse se, lagaatar X-rays aa rahi thi. Woh jagah Earth se lagbhag 380 crore light year door thi. Aur wahan jo ho raha tha, woh 100 arab Suns se bhi zyaada chamak raha tha. California ke astronomer Joshua Bloom aur unki team ne, Hubble aur Chandra telescope us taraf ghumaaye. Pata chala, ki chamak ek shaant galaxy ke bilkul beech se aa rahi thi. Wahan ek bada black hole tha, jo hamari galaxy ke black hole se shayad do guna bhaari tha. Woh lambe samay se chup baitha tha. Phir, hamare Sun jaisa ek taara, bhatakta hua uske bahut paas chala gaya.

CHAPTER: TORN APART
Black hole kisi taare ko, andar nigle bina bhi tod sakta hai. Yeh idea 1975 mein astrophysicist Jack Hills ne diya tha. Har bhaari black hole ke chaaron taraf ek andekhi seema hoti hai, jise tidal radius kehte hain. Is seema ke andar, gravity ka khel badal jaata hai. Taare ka jo hissa black hole ke paas hota hai, us par khinchaav, door wale hisse se kahin zyaada hota hai. Yahi farq taare ko kheench kar lamba kar deta hai. Kisi insaan ke saath bhi yahi hota. Pairon par khinchaav, sir se kahin zyaada hota, aur shareer kheench kar ek patli lakeer ban jaata. 1988 mein, Stephen Hawking ne apni kitaab, A Brief History of Time, mein ise ek mazedaar naam diya. Spaghettification. 2011 mein, us taare ke saath bhi yahi hua. Taare ki apni gravity haar gayi. Pehle woh gol se ek boond jaisa bana. Phir ek lambi lakeer. Aur kuch hi ghanton mein, woh poori tarah bikhar gaya. Lekin black hole poore taare ko ek saath nahi nigalta. Taare ki lagbhag aadhi gas, hazaron kilometre per second ki raftaar se, wapas space mein phenk di gayi. Baaki aadhi gas uske chaaron taraf ghoomne lagi. Tez ghoomti gas aapas mein ragad kar, lakhon degree tak garam ho gayi. Aur roshni ka ek chamakta chakka bana, jise accretion disk kehte hain. Usi waqt, uske magnetic field ne kuch gas ko pakda, aur use do seedhi dhaaron mein baahar phenka. In dhaaron ko jets kehte hain. Inki raftaar thi, light ki speed ka 99.5 percent. Aur ittefaq se, ek jet ka rukh seedha Earth ki taraf tha. Isiliye yeh chamak itni tez dikhi. Aise toote hue taare pehle bhi dikhe the. Lekin yeh pehli baar tha, jab us tabahi ka jet seedha Earth ki taraf tha.

CHAPTER: THE BLACK HOLE THAT BURPED
Lekin 2011 wala black hole, brahmand ke sabse bade shikaariyon ke saamne kuch bhi nahi tha. Aur kuch shikaariyon ki harkatein, scientists ko aaj bhi hairaan karti hain. October 2018. Ek telescope survey ne, lagbhag 66.5 crore light year door, ek aur chamak dekhi. Ek aur black hole ne ek taare ko toda tha. Kuch mahino mein chamak shaant ho gayi, aur scientists aage badh gaye. Lekin June 2021 mein, lagbhag teen saal baad, Harvard ki astronomer Yvette Cendes aur unki team ne, New Mexico ke Very Large Array radio telescope se, us jagah ko phir se dekha. Black hole phir se jaag chuka tha. Bina kisi naye taare ke, woh light ki aadhi raftaar se, gas baahar phenk raha tha. Cendes ne kaha, ki yeh aisa hai, jaise khaana khaane ke saalon baad, kisi ko achanak dakaar aa jaaye. Scientists ne is chamak ko mazaak mein naam diya, Jetty McJetface. Aaj tak pakka nahi pata, ki black hole ne itne saal yeh gas kahan roke rakhi.

CHAPTER: THE GIANT THAT SWALLOWS WHOLE
Ab sabse bade shikaariyon mein se ek ki baat. Uska naam hai, TON 618. 1957 mein, Mexico ki Tonantzintla Observatory mein, do astronomers, Braulio Iriarte aur Enrique Chavira, aasmaan mein neeli roshni wale taaron ki list bana rahe the. Unki list mein, 618 number par ek dhundhli neeli roshni thi. Tab kisi ko nahi pata tha, ki woh kya hai. 1970 mein, Italy ke Bologna ke radio telescope ne dikhaaya, ki woh taara nahi, ek quasar hai. Ek aisi jagah, jahan ek bahut bada black hole lagaatar gas kha raha hai, aur us garam gas ki roshni, poori galaxy se zyaada hai. 2004 mein, astronomers ne iska wazan naapa. Kuch andaazon ke hisaab se, yeh hamare Sun se lagbhag 6,600 crore guna bhaari hai. Ise ultramassive black hole kaha jaata hai. Iska event horizon, yaani woh seema jahan se roshni bhi wapas nahi aa sakti, lagbhag 2,600 astronomical unit chauda hai. Ek astronomical unit, yaani Earth se Sun ki doori. Neptune, Sun se sirf 30 unit door hai. Agar TON 618 ko Sun ki jagah rakh diya jaaye, to poora Solar System uske andar sama jaaye. Aur roshni ko iske ek kinaare se doosre kinaare tak jaane mein, lagbhag do hafte lagenge. Iske chaaron taraf ghoomti gas, lagbhag 140 lakh crore Suns jitni roshni deti hai. Lekin aise bade black holes ki ek ajeeb baat hai. Woh taaron ko todte nahi. Black hole jitna bhaari hota hai, uska event horizon utni hi tezi se bada hota hai. Lekin tidal radius utni tezi se nahi badhta. Jab black hole Sun se lagbhag 10 crore guna bhaari ho jaata hai, to tidal radius, event horizon ke andar chala jaata hai. Matlab, hamare Sun jaisa taara agar TON 618 ke paas jaaye, to woh tootega nahi. Koi chamak nahi hogi. TON 618 use poora ka poora, chupchaap nigal jaayega. Brahmand ke sabse bade shikaari, bilkul khamoshi se shikaar karte hain.

CHAPTER: THE HUNTER AT HOME
TON 618, Earth se 1,000 crore light year se bhi zyaada door hai. Lekin ek bada black hole, hamari apni galaxy ke beech mein bhi hai. 1974 mein, American astronomers Bruce Balick aur Robert Brown ne, Green Bank ke radio telescopes se, Milky Way ke beech mein ek tez radio signal pakda. Woh jagah Earth se lagbhag 26,000 light year door hai. Baad mein use naam mila, Sagittarius A star. Agle tees saal tak, Andrea Ghez aur Reinhard Genzel ki teams ne, Keck aur Very Large Telescope se, us jagah ke chaaron taraf ghoomte taaron ko dekha. Ek taara, S2, us andekhi jagah ke paas se guzarte waqt, lagbhag 7,700 kilometre per second ki raftaar pakad leta hai. Light ki speed ka lagbhag 2.5 percent. In taaron ki chaal ne saabit kiya, ki wahan ek black hole hai, jo Sun se 40 lakh guna bhaari hai. Is khoj ke liye, 2020 mein Ghez aur Genzel ko Physics ka Nobel Prize mila. Aur May 2022 mein, Event Horizon Telescope ne iski pehli tasveer dikhaayi. Aaj yeh black hole lagbhag shaant hai. Iske paas khaane ke liye zyaada gas nahi hai. Lekin hamesha aisa nahi tha.

CHAPTER: A FLASH 200 YEARS AGO
June 2023. France ki Strasbourg Observatory ke astronomer Frederic Marin aur unki team ne, NASA ke IXPE satellite ka data dekha. Sagittarius A star ke paas, gas ke badal X-rays mein chamak rahe the. Jab unhone us roshni ka polarization naapa, to pata chala, ki yeh badalon ki apni roshni nahi thi. Yeh black hole ki ek purani chamak thi, jo badalon se takra kar ab Earth tak pahunch rahi thi. Hisaab se, lagbhag 200 saal pehle, yeh black hole kuch samay ke liye jaag utha tha. Shayad usne paas aaye kisi gas ke badal ko kha liya tha. Lagbhag ek saal tak, uski chamak aaj se kam se kam 10 lakh guna zyaada thi. Phir woh dobara so gaya. Scientists ke andaaze se, hamari galaxy mein har 10,000 se 1 lakh saal mein ek baar, koi bhatakta taara is black hole ke paas aakar toot jaata hai.

ENDING
Lekin is shaant black hole ka asli roop, shayad tab dikhega, jab hamari galaxy ki sabse badi takkar hogi. Padosi galaxy Andromeda, lagbhag 110 kilometre per second ki raftaar se, hamari taraf aa rahi hai. Pehle maana jaata tha, ki lagbhag 400 crore saal baad, dono galaxies zaroor takrayengi. Lekin 2025 ki ek study ke hisaab se, agle 1,000 crore saal mein takkar ka chance, lagbhag aadha hai. Agar takkar hui, to arabon taare aur gas ke bade badal, galaxy ke beech ki taraf girenge. Sagittarius A star, aur Andromeda ka black hole, ek doosre ke chakkar lagaate hue, aakhir mein ek ho jaayenge. Tab shayad yeh soya hua black hole poori tarah jaag uthe. Ek ke baad ek, taare toot sakte hain, aur galaxy ke beech mein ek quasar jal sakta hai. 2011 mein, Swift ne jo dekha tha, woh sirf ek door ki kahaani nahi thi. Woh shayad hamari galaxy ke aane wale kal ki ek jhalak thi. Aasmaan mein taare shaant dikhte hain. Lekin us andhere mein, gravity ka khel lagaatar chal raha hai. Aur brahmand ke shikaari, bas agle bhatakte taare ka intezaar kar rahe hain.
