OPENING
2006 mein space mein ek aisa dhamaka hua, jise NASA ke ek satellite ne 102 second tak pakda. Usse itni energy nikli jitni hamara Sooraj apni poori life mein nahi chhodta. Duniya ki sabse badi telescopes ne turant wahan dekha, tab kuch scientists ne ek ajeeb naam liya, white hole, jo black hole ka bilkul ulta hota hai. Lekin kya white holes sach mein exist karte hain?

CHAPTER: THE BLACK HOLE PLAYED BACKWARDS
Jab ek bahut bada taara apna saara fuel khatam kar deta hai, toh woh apne hi weight se andar girne lagta hai aur itna dab jaata hai ki uski gravity se light bhi bahar nahi nikal paati. Yahi black hole hota hai, jiske chaaron taraf event horizon naam ki ek boundary hoti hai jiske andar jaane ke baad kuch bhi wapas nahi aa sakta. 1915 mein Einstein ne gravity ke equations likhe the, aur black hole unhi equations se nikla, lekin inme ek ajeeb baat chhupi thi. Yeh equations time ke aage chalne aur peeche chalne, dono mein bilkul ek jaise kaam karte hain. Agar cricket match ka replay ulta chalaya jaaye, toh ball wicketkeeper ke gloves se nikal kar seedha bowler ke haath mein chali jaati hai, aur physics ke hisaab se dono video bilkul sahi lagte hain. Isi tarah black hole ka replay ulta chalaya jaaye toh milta hai white hole, ek aisi boundary jahan se light aur matter sirf bahar aate hain aur andar kuch nahi ja sakta. 1960 mein Martin Kruskal aur George Szekeres ne jab black hole ke spacetime ka poora mathematical map banaya, toh equations mein uska yeh doosra hissa bhi chhupa hua mila. Maths ne white hole ko allow kar diya tha, phir bhi zyaadatar scientists ne ise maanne se mana kar diya.

CHAPTER: TOO STRANGE TO EXIST
Unka pehla doubt time ki direction par tha, kyunki kitchen mein doodh ubal kar gir toh jaata hai, lekin gira hua doodh kabhi khud wapas patile mein nahi jaata. Nature mein cheezein failti hain, khud wapas ikattha nahi hoti, jabki white hole bilkul wahi ulta kaam karta hai jo hamne kabhi hote hue nahi dekha. Phir bhi 1964 mein Russia ke scientist Igor Novikov ne kaha ki white holes exist kar sakte hain, lekin das saal baad Douglas Eardley ne isme ek badi problem dikhayi. White hole ke andar kuch ja toh nahi sakta, lekin uski gravity ki wajah se space ka dust aur radiation uski boundary ke bahar jama hone lagta hai. Eardley ke hisaab se yeh jama hua matter itna bhaari ho jaata hai ki poora white hole khud collapse ho kar black hole ban jaata hai. Matlab yeh ban bhi jaaye, toh tik nahi paayega. Usi saal, 1974 mein Mumbai ke TIFR mein baithe Jayant Narlikar aur unki team ne ek alag angle rakha, aur famous journal Nature mein likha ki white holes space ki sabse tez radiation ki wajah ban sakte hain. Unke liye yeh space ke sabse bade explosions ko samajhne ka ek bada clue tha, lekin kai saalon tak kisi ne aisa koi dhamaka pakda hi nahi. Phir 2006 mein Swift satellite ne woh ajeeb flash dekhi.

CHAPTER: THE BURST WITHOUT A STAR
Space mein kabhi kabhi gamma rays ki ek bahut tez flash aati hai, jise gamma ray burst kehte hain, aur yeh do tarah ke hote hain. Chhote wale do second se kam chalte hain aur do mare hue taaron ke takraane se bante hain, jabki lambe wale ek bade taare ke marne se bante hain aur unke baad hamesha ek supernova ki light dikhti hai. 2006 ka woh dhamaka 102 second chala, yaani lamba wala tha, lekin uske baad koi supernova nahi mila. Scientists ne use hybrid kaha kyunki woh kisi bhi category mein fit nahi hota tha. 2011 mein Alon Retter aur Shlomo Heller ne ek alag idea diya ki yeh shayad ek white hole tha, jo Diwali ke anaar ki tarah ek hi jhatke mein apna saara matter bahar nikaal kar khatam ho gaya. Unhone ise Small Bang naam diya, lekin story mein ek twist tab aaya jab baad mein scientists ne isi dhamake ki purani light dobara check ki aur unhe kilonova ke signs mile. Kilonova woh halki light hoti hai jo do mare hue taaron, yaani neutron stars ke takraane ke baad aati hai, isliye zyaadatar astronomers maante hain ki yeh white hole nahi, balki do neutron stars ki takkar thi. Koi pakka proof nahi mila, lekin white hole ka idea ek alag raaste se wapas laut aaya.

CHAPTER: WHEN A BLACK HOLE TURNS WHITE
Ab baat karte hain black hole ke bilkul centre ki, jahan Einstein ke equations kehte hain ki saara matter ek chhote point mein dab jaata hai aur density infinite ho jaati hai. Lekin jahan koi number infinite hota hai, wahan physics fail hoti hai, kyunki itne chhote scale par quantum physics chalti hai aur gravity ko quantum physics se jodne wali theory abhi tak poori nahi bani. 2014 mein Carlo Rovelli aur Hal Haggard ne suggest kiya ki shayad matter us point tak kabhi pahunchta hi nahi. Dabte dabte ek limit par woh wapas bounce kar jaata hai, bilkul waise jaise ek spring ko poora dabaya jaaye toh woh zor se wapas khulti hai, aur yahi bounce black hole ko white hole bana deta hai. Yahan saara khel time ka hai, kyunki black hole ke aas paas time bahut slow chalta hai. Andar ke matter ke liye yeh bounce bas ek second ka hota hai, lekin bahar se dekhne walon ke liye isme billions of years lag sakte hain, isliye aaj ke black holes abhi tak white hole nahi bane. Aur yahin ek bada Indian naam aata hai, Abhay Ashtekar, jo loop quantum gravity ke founders mein se ek hain. 2018 mein unhone Javier Olmedo aur Parampreet Singh ke saath dikhaya ki is theory mein black hole ke beech woh infinite density wala point bachta hi nahi, balki wahan ek bridge banta hai jo black hole ko uske opposite form, white hole se jod deta hai.

CHAPTER: HIDDEN IN PLAIN SIGHT
Agar black holes sach mein white holes ban jaate hain, toh yeh change sabse pehle sabse chhote black holes mein hoga, kyunki unme mass kam hota hai, toh isi wajah se unka bounce jaldi ho jaayega. Kuch scientists ke hisaab se Big Bang ke turant baad aise bahut chhote black holes bane honge, jo billions of years mein white holes ban chuke honge aur unke bache hue pieces shayad aaj bhi space mein ghoom rahe hain. Ek piece ka size atom se bhi kahin chhota aur weight ret ke ek chhote daane jitna hota hai, jo na chamakte hain aur na kisi cheez se takraate hain. Unka pata sirf gravity se chalta hai, bilkul dark matter ki tarah jo galaxies ko jod kar rakhta hai lekin dikhta nahi, isliye Carlo Rovelli aur unke saathiyon ne suggest kiya ki dark matter ka kuch hissa aise white holes ho sakte hain. Yeh abhi sirf ek idea hai, lekin ek theory isse bhi badi hai. Big Bang mein saara matter aur energy ek jagah se bahar aaye aur andar kuch nahi gaya, aur white hole bhi bilkul yahi karta hai, isliye kuch physicists maante hain ki hamara Big Bang khud ek bahut bade white hole jaisa tha.

ENDING
Toh white holes aakhir hain kya? Black holes ki aakhri stage, Big Bang ka twin, ya sirf maths jise nature kabhi banne nahi deta? Science abhi tak yeh decide nahi kar paayi hai, lekin jab bhi space mein bina taare ka koi dhamaka dikhta hai, toh scientists wahi 2006 wala sawaal phir se uthaate hain.
