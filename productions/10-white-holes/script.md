## Script
OPENING
Antariksh mein ek dhamaka hua, jisne kuch hi seconds mein utni energy chhodi, jitni hamara Sooraj apni poori zindagi mein deta hai. NASA ke Swift satellite ne use pakda, aur woh dhamaka ek sau do second tak chalta raha. Itna lamba dhamaka aam taur par ek marte hue taare se aata hai, jise supernova kehte hain. Duniya ki sabse badi telescopes ne wahan dekha. Koi supernova nahi tha. Tab kuch scientists ne ek ajeeb sawaal uthaya. Kya yeh ek white hole tha, black hole ka bilkul ulta, jahan se cheezein sirf bahar aati hain?

CH1
Ise samajhne ke liye, pehle black hole ko samajhna hoga. Jab ek bahut bada taara apna saara fuel jala deta hai, toh woh apne hi wazan se andar girne lagta hai. Woh itna dab jaata hai, ki uski gravity se roshni bhi bahar nahi nikal paati. Yahi hai black hole. Uske chaaron taraf ek seema hoti hai, jise event horizon kehte hain. Rush hour mein Mumbai local ke darwaze jaisa, andar ghusna aasaan, bahar nikalna mushkil. Bas black hole mein bahar nikalna mushkil nahi, namumkin hai.
Unnees sau pandrah mein Einstein ne gravity ke equations likhe, aur black hole unhi equations se nikla. Lekin in equations mein ek ajeeb baat hai. Yeh time ke aage chalne aur peeche chalne, dono mein ek jaise kaam karte hain. Agar cricket match ka replay ulta chalaya jaaye, toh ball wicketkeeper ke gloves se nikal kar, wapas bowler ke haath mein pahunch jaati hai. Physics ke hisaab se, dono video sahi lagte hain.
Black hole ka replay ulta chalaya jaaye, toh milta hai white hole. Ek aisi seema, jahan se roshni aur matter sirf bahar aate hain, aur andar kuch nahi ja sakta. Jaise match khatam hone ke baad stadium ka exit gate, jahan bheed sirf bahar aati hai.
Unnees sau saath mein Martin Kruskal aur George Szekeres ne black hole ka poora naksha banaya. Us naksha mein ek doosra hissa bhi chhupa tha. Ek white hole. Maths ne use ijaazat de di thi. Phir bhi, zyaadatar scientists ne ise maanne se mana kar diya.

CH2
Pehla shak time ki disha par tha. Rasoi mein doodh ubal kar gir jaata hai, lekin gira hua doodh kabhi khud wapas patile mein nahi chadhta. Nature mein cheezein bikharti hain, khud ba khud simatti nahi. White hole wahi ulta kaam karta hai, jo hamne kabhi hote hue nahi dekha.
Phir bhi, unnees sau chaunsath mein Russia ke scientist Igor Novikov ne kaha ki white holes ho sakte hain. Das saal baad, Douglas Eardley ne ek badi dikkat dikhayi. White hole se matter bahar aata hai, lekin bahar se bhi kuch na kuch uski taraf girta rehta hai. Jaise ek tang gali mein do taraf se bheed aa jaaye, aur jaam lag jaaye. Eardley ke hisaab se yeh jaam itna bhaari ho jaata hai, ki white hole khud dhah kar black hole ban jaata hai. Matlab, white hole bane bhi, toh tik nahi paayega.
Usi saal, Bharat mein ek alag soch chal rahi thi. Jayant Narlikar, K M V Apparao aur Naresh Dadhich ne mashhoor journal Nature mein likha, ki white holes antariksh ki sabse tez radiation ki wajah ban sakte hain. Unke liye white hole ek suraag tha, antariksh ke sabse bade dhamakon ko samajhne ka. Lekin dashakon tak kisi ne aisa koi dhamaka pakda hi nahi. Phir do hazaar chhah mein, Swift satellite ne woh ajeeb dhamaka dekha.

CH3
Antariksh mein kabhi kabhi gamma rays ki ek bahut tez chamak aati hai. Ise gamma-ray burst kehte hain. Yeh do tarah ke hote hain. Chhote wale do second se kam chalte hain, aur do mare hue taaron ke takraane se bante hain. Lambe wale ek bade taare ke marne se bante hain, aur unke baad hamesha ek supernova ki chamak dikhti hai.
Do hazaar chhah ka woh burst ek sau do second chala. Matlab lamba wala. Lekin supernova nahi mila. Scientists ne use hybrid kaha, kyunki woh kisi bhi khaane mein fit nahi hota tha.
Do hazaar gyaarah mein Alon Retter aur Shlomo Heller ne ek alag idea diya. Unke hisaab se yeh white hole ho sakta tha, jo ek hi jhatke mein apna saara matter bahar phenk kar khatam ho gaya. Diwali ke anaar ki tarah, jo ek saath saari chingaariyan bahar ugal deta hai. Unhone ise naam diya, Small Bang.
Lekin kahani mein ek mod hai. Baad mein scientists ne isi burst ki purani roshni dobara jaanchi, aur unhe kilonova ke nishaan mile. Kilonova woh halki chamak hai, jo do mare hue taaron ke takraane ke baad aati hai. Isliye zyaadatar astronomers maante hain, ki yeh white hole nahi, shayad do neutron stars ka takraav tha. Saboot nahi mila. Lekin white hole ka idea, ek alag raaste se wapas laut aaya.

CH4
Ab baat black hole ke bilkul beech ki. Einstein ke equations kehte hain, wahan saara matter ek bindu mein dab jaata hai, aur density anant ho jaati hai. Ise singularity kehte hain. Lekin jahan koi number anant ho jaaye, wahan physics fail ho rahi hoti hai. Itne chhote paimaane par quantum physics chalti hai, aur gravity ko quantum physics se jodne wali theory abhi tak poori nahi bani.
Do hazaar chaudah mein Carlo Rovelli aur Hal Haggard ne ek idea diya. Shayad matter us bindu tak kabhi pahunchta hi nahi. Dabte dabte ek hadd par woh wapas uchhal jaata hai, jaise ek spring ko poora dabaya jaaye, toh woh zor se wapas khulti hai. Aur yahi uchhaal black hole ko white hole bana deta hai.
Yahan time ka khel hai. Black hole ke aas paas time bahut dheere chalta hai. Andar ke matter ke liye yeh uchhaal ek pal ka hai, lekin bahar se dekhne walon ke liye arabon saal lag sakte hain. Isliye aaj ke black holes abhi tak white hole nahi bane.
Aur yahan ek Bharatiya naam aata hai. Abhay Ashtekar loop quantum gravity naam ki theory par kaam karte hain, wahi theory jo kehti hai ki universe ka ant ek uchhaal bhi ho sakta hai. Do hazaar atthaarah mein unhone Javier Olmedo aur Parampreet Singh ke saath dikhaya, ki is theory mein black hole ke beech singularity bachti hi nahi. Wahan ek pul banta hai, jo black hole ko uske ulte roop, white hole, se jod deta hai.

CH5
Agar black holes sach mein white holes ban jaate hain, toh yeh badlaav sabse pehle sabse chhote black holes mein hoga. Kuch scientists ke hisaab se, Big Bang ke turant baad bahut chhote black holes bane honge. Arabon saal mein woh white holes ban chuke honge, aur unke bache hue tukde shayad aaj bhi antariksh mein ghoom rahe hain.
Ek tukde ka size atom se bhi kahin chhota, aur wazan ret ke ek bareek daane jitna. Woh na chamakte hain, na kisi cheez se takraate hain. Unka pata sirf gravity se chalta hai, bilkul dark matter ki tarah, jo galaxies ko jod kar rakhta hai, lekin dikhta nahi. Carlo Rovelli aur unke saathiyon ne yahi suggest kiya hai, ki dark matter ka kuch hissa aise white holes ho sakte hain. Yeh abhi sirf ek idea hai.
Aur ek khayal isse bhi bada hai. Big Bang mein saara matter aur energy ek jagah se bahar aaye, aur andar kuch nahi gaya. White hole bhi yahi karta hai. Isliye kuch physicists kehte hain, ki hamara Big Bang khud ek bahut bade white hole jaisa tha.

ENDING
Toh white holes kya hain? Black holes ka aakhri padaav, Big Bang ka judwa, ya sirf maths, jise nature kabhi banne nahi deta? Science abhi tak faisla nahi kar paayi. Lekin jab bhi antariksh mein koi aisa dhamaka dikhta hai, jo kisi taare se nahi aata, toh scientists wahi sawaal phir se uthaate hain, jo do hazaar chhah mein uthaya gaya tha.
