# Voice-clone recording script (about 3.5–4 minutes)

## Before recording
- Use a quiet room with soft furniture (a bedroom, or a wardrobe full of clothes). Turn the fan and AC off; no music, no TV.
- Hold the phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side. **Keep the same distance the whole time.**
- Stay silent for 2 seconds at the start, then begin.
- Record everything **in one file**. If you make a mistake, pause for 2 seconds and re-read that whole sentence calmly. Don't stop the recording; I will cut the mistakes out.
- Read only the narration. Skip the headings and the `( )` notes.

## How to read
- **Calm, slow and low**, like a documentary narrator telling a story late at night. A little slower than your normal speaking speed.
- Speak from the chest, not the throat. Relax, and don't "perform" or sound excited.
- Pause briefly at commas, longer at full stops, and longest at `...`.
- Say the English words (galaxy, telescope, gravity…) the way you'd naturally say them in Hindi conversation.

### Part 1 (calm, steady)

Saal 1933. California Institute of Technology mein, Swiss astronomer Fritz Zwicky ek ajeeb hisaab mein uljhe hue the. Lagbhag 30 crore light-years door, hazaar se zyaada galaxies ka ek vishaal jhund tha: Coma Cluster. Zwicky ne in galaxies ki raftaar ka hisaab lagaya, aur kuch gadbad nikli.

Galaxies itni tezi se bhaag rahi thin, ki dikhne wale saare taaron ki gravity milkar bhi unhe baandh kar nahi rakh sakti thi. Cluster ko kab ka bikhar jaana chahiye tha. Phir bhi woh tika hua tha... jaise koi adrishya haath use thaame hue ho.

### Part 2 (clear, informative: numbers and science words)

Milky Way lagbhag 1 lakh light-years chaudi hai, aur usme 100 arab se zyaada taare hain. Sun, galaxy ke center ke charon taraf lagbhag 230 kilometer prati second ki raftaar se chakkar laga raha hai. Roshni ek second mein lagbhag 3 lakh kilometer ka safar tay karti hai. Phir bhi, Sun se Earth tak pahunchne mein use 8 minute aur 20 second lag jaate hain.

Brahmand ke kul matter ka lagbhag 85 percent, dark matter hai. Normal matter sirf 5 percent, dark matter 27 percent, aur baaki 68 percent, dark energy. Hubble, James Webb, radio telescopes aur X-ray telescopes, sab milkar bhi ise dekh nahi paate.

### Part 3 (tension: slightly more weight, same calm voice)

Arabon light-years door, do vishaal galaxy clusters aapas mein takraaye. Takkar ke baad, chhota cluster lagbhag 4,700 kilometer prati second ki raftaar se aage nikal raha hai. Garam gas beech mein hi atak gayi... lekin asal mass, bina ruke, ek-doosre ke aar-paar nikal chuka tha.

Ek black hole ke paas, gravity itni taakatwar hoti hai ki roshni bhi wahan se bach kar nahi nikal paati. Jo bhi us seema ko paar karta hai, woh hamesha ke liye brahmand se ojhal ho jaata hai.

### Part 4 (wonder: softer, a sense of awe)

Big Bang ke baad, lagbhag 3 lakh 80 hazaar saal tak, poora brahmand ek garam, chamakte kohre jaisa tha. Phir dheere-dheere, gravity ne matter ko jodna shuru kiya. Dhaagon aur gaanthon ka ek vishaal jaal bana, jise cosmic web kehte hain. Wahin pehle taare jale. Wahin pehli galaxies bani. Aur arabon saal baad, unhi taaron ki raakh se, grah bane... aur zindagi.

### Part 5 (quiet, reflective: slowest part)

Brahmand ka zyaadatar hissa aaj bhi andhere mein chhupa hai. Har jawab, ek naya sawaal le kar aata hai. Shayad brahmand ki sabse badi khoj abhi baaki hai.

Aur jis din yeh rahasya khulega... us din insaan shayad yeh bhi samajh paayega, ki is vishaal brahmand mein, uski asal jagah kahaan hai.
