# Production 02: 10 Facts You Don't Know About Black Holes | Hindi Documentary

Channel rules (packaging, narration style, edit method): see [`../README.md`](../README.md).

- The narration is **third-person storytelling**: one continuous story from 1783 to 2026. It never addresses the viewer and never announces numbers.
- The "10 facts" countdown lives **only on screen**, as English chapter cards (`FACT #10 …`).
- Story spine: Karl Schwarzschild finds the "point of no return" in a war trench (1915). The countdown follows the idea from a village priest (1783) to Webb's 2026 discovery of a black hole older than its own galaxy, and the ending returns to Schwarzschild.

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:55)

> December 1915. Russia ke morche par, German army ka ek lieutenant, top ke golon ka raasta naapne ka hisaab lagata tha. Uska naam tha Karl Schwarzschild, Germany ke sabse mashhoor astronomers mein se ek.
>
> Jang ke shor ke beech, Schwarzschild ne Einstein ki bilkul nayi theory, General Relativity, ke equations ka pehla sateek hal nikaal liya. Einstein khud hairaan the.
>
> Lekin us hal mein ek ajeeb seema chhupi thi. Agar kisi bhi cheez ka saara mass ek chhote se gole mein simat jaaye, to us gole se kuch bhi wapas nahi aa sakta. Roshni bhi nahi. Sun ke liye yeh gola sirf chhah kilometer chauda hota. Earth ke liye, ek kanche jitna.
>
> Paanch mahine baad, Schwarzschild ki maut ho gayi. Woh kabhi nahi jaan paaye, ki unke hisaab mein brahmaand ki sabse ajeeb cheez chhupi thi... Black Hole.
>
> Aur sau saal se bhi zyaada baad, 2026 mein, James Webb Space Telescope ne ek aisa black hole dekha, jo shayad apni galaxy se bhi pehle paida hua tha.

### FACT #10: Predicted in 1783, by a village priest (0:55–1:55)

> Lekin yeh khayal Schwarzschild se bhi purana tha.
>
> 1783 mein, England ke ek chhote se gaon Thornhill ke paadri, John Michell ne apne dost Henry Cavendish ko ek lamba khat likha. Michell bhookampon ka adhyayan bhi karte the, aur unka banaya ek taraazu, baad mein Earth ka wazan naapne ke kaam aaya.
>
> Michell ne Newton ki gravity se ek seedha sa sawaal poocha. Agar koi taara Sun jitna hi ghana ho, lekin usse paanch sau guna bada, to kya hoga? Jawab chaunkane wala tha. Us taare ki gravity se bachne ke liye, roshni ki raftaar bhi kam padti. Aisa taara poori tarah adrishya hota.
>
> Michell ne inhe "dark stars" kaha. Aur likha, ki aise taaron ko sirf unke aas-paas ghoomte doosre taaron ki chaal se pehchaana ja sakta hai. Lagbhag do sau saal baad, astronomers ne theek yahi tareeka apnaya.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #10 · PREDICTED IN 1783, BY A VILLAGE PRIEST` · `1783` · `John Michell` · `Thornhill, England` · `500× wider than the Sun` · `"DARK STARS"`
- **Visuals:** old-paper `1783` card; a bright star; graphic: light rising from a star and falling back; a star orbiting an invisible companion.

### FACT #9: Even Einstein didn't believe in them (1:55–3:05)

> Khud Einstein ko bhi Schwarzschild ki is seema par yakeen nahi tha. 1939 mein unhone ek research paper likha, yeh saabit karne ke liye, ki aisi cheezein asal duniya mein ho hi nahi saktin.
>
> Usi saal, California mein, J. Robert Oppenheimer aur unke student Hartland Snyder bilkul ulta nateeja nikaal rahe the. Unhone dikhaya, ki jab ek bahut bhaari taare ka eendhan khatm hota hai, to woh apne hi wazan se dabta chala jaata hai, aur kabhi nahi rukta. Aakhir mein woh baahar ki duniya se hamesha ke liye kat jaata hai.
>
> Unka paper 1 September 1939 ko chhapa. Usi din Germany ne Poland par hamla kiya, aur Doosra Vishwa Yuddh shuru ho gaya. Duniya ka dhyaan kahin aur tha. Kuch saal baad Oppenheimer atom bomb ke project ke pramukh bane, aur is vishay par kabhi nahi laute.
>
> In cheezon ko naam milne mein aur 28 saal lage. 1967 mein, American physicist John Wheeler ne ek lecture mein inhe ek chhota sa naam diya, jo hamesha ke liye chipak gaya... Black Hole.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #9 · EVEN EINSTEIN DIDN'T BELIEVE IN THEM` · `1939` · `J. Robert Oppenheimer` · `Oppenheimer & Snyder` · `1 SEPTEMBER 1939` · `World War II begins` · `1967` · `John Wheeler` · `"BLACK HOLE"`
- **Visuals:** a massive star; graphic: a star collapsing into a point; dark stormy nebula on the war line; a black hole visualization on "Black Hole".

### FACT #8: The first one was found by its X-rays (3:05–4:20)

> Lekin jis cheez se roshni bhi bahar na aaye, use dhoondha kaise jaaye? Astronomers ke saamne yahi sawaal tha. Jawab X-rays mein mila.
>
> 1964 mein, America ke New Mexico se chhode gaye ek chhote se rocket ne, Cygnus taramandal mein X-rays ka ek tez strot pakda... Cygnus X-One. 1972 mein pata chala, ki wahan ek vishaal neela taara, har lagbhag chhah din mein, kisi adrishya saathi ke charon taraf ghoom raha hai. Woh saathi Sun se kai guna bhaari tha, lekin dikhta nahi tha. Uski gravity neele taare ki gas kheench rahi thi, aur girti hui gas itni garam ho jaati thi, ki X-rays chhodne lagti thi. Theek waise hi, jaise Michell ne likha tha, ek adrishya cheez apne saathi ki chaal se pakdi gayi.
>
> Stephen Hawking ne apne dost Kip Thorne se shart lagayi, ki Cygnus X-One black hole nahi hai. Baad mein Hawking ne maana, ki yeh ek "insurance" thi. Agar black holes na nikalte, to kam se kam shart to jeet jaate. 1990 mein, Hawking ne haar maan li.
>
> Aaj ki naap ke mutaabik, Cygnus X-One lagbhag 7,200 light-years door hai, aur Sun se 21 guna bhaari. Insaniyat ka pehla pehchaana gaya black hole.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #8 · THE FIRST ONE WAS FOUND BY ITS X-RAYS` · `1964` · `CYGNUS X-1` · `1972` · `A blue supergiant, orbiting every 5.6 days` · `Hawking vs Thorne` · `1990: Hawking concedes` · `7,200 light-years` · `21 × the Sun's mass`
- **Visuals:** Cygnus constellation / Milky Way star field; X-ray binary animation (blue star feeding a disk around a black hole); X-ray sky.

### FACT #7: Black holes are not completely black (4:20–5:25)

> Black hole se kuch bhi bahar nahi aa sakta. Kam se kam, sab yahi maante the. 1974 mein, Stephen Hawking ne is niyam mein ek daraar dhoondh li.
>
> Hawking ne Einstein ki gravity ko, bahut chhoti cheezon ki duniya, yaani quantum physics, ke saath milaya. Nateeja chaunkane wala tha. Black hole ke kinare, yaani event horizon ke paas se, ek bahut halki si chamak lagataar bahar nikalti hai. Aaj ise kehte hain... Hawking radiation.
>
> Iska matlab tha, ki black hole bhi hamesha nahi rehte. Woh dheere-dheere apni energy kho kar, ek din bhaap ban kar ud jaate hain. Lekin bahut, bahut dheere. Sun jitne bhaari black hole ko khatm hone mein itne saal lagenge, ki us sankhya mein ek ke baad sadsath shoonya honge.
>
> Hawking radiation aaj tak kabhi dekhi nahi gayi. Itni dheemi chamak ko pakadna, aaj ki kisi bhi machine ke bas mein nahi. Shayad isi wajah se, Stephen Hawking ko kabhi Nobel Prize nahi mila.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #7 · BLACK HOLES ARE NOT COMPLETELY BLACK` · `1974` · `Stephen Hawking` · `Gravity + Quantum Physics` · `EVENT HORIZON` · `HAWKING RADIATION` · `10⁶⁷ years` (big) · `Age of the universe: 1.38 × 10¹⁰ years` · `Never observed`
- **Visuals:** black hole visualizations with a faint glow at the horizon; graphic: particles escaping from the horizon; a fading black hole.

### FACT #6: They stretch stars like spaghetti (5:25–6:30)

> Black hole ke paas gravity sirf tez nahi hoti. Woh baraabar bhi nahi hoti.
>
> Girti hui kisi bhi cheez ka jo hissa black hole ke zyaada paas hota hai, use kahin zyaada zor se kheencha jaata hai. Woh cheez lambi aur patli hoti chali jaati hai, jaise spaghetti. Scientists ise yahi naam dete hain... spaghettification.
>
> Aur door se dekhne waalon ko, girti hui cheez event horizon ke paas dheemi padti dikhti hai, jaise samay khud ruk gaya ho. Uski roshni laal hoti jaati hai, phir dhundhli, aur aakhir mein gaayab.
>
> 2019 mein, yeh sirf kalpana nahi raha. Lagbhag 21 crore light-years door, Sun jaisa ek taara, ek supermassive black hole ke bahut paas se guzra. Black hole ne use cheer kar ek lambi dhaar mein badal diya, aur uska lagbhag aadha mass nigal gaya. Chile ke Very Large Telescope samet kai telescopes ne, chhah mahine tak is maut ko record kiya. Kisi taare ki aisi maut, itne paas se, pehle kabhi nahi dekhi gayi thi.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #6 · THEY STRETCH STARS LIKE SPAGHETTI` · `"SPAGHETTIFICATION"` · `Time appears to freeze` · `2019` · `AT2019qiz` · `215 million light-years` · `A Sun-like star` · `Half its mass swallowed` · `Very Large Telescope, Chile`
- **Visuals:** tidal disruption animations (star stretched into a stream); VLT in Chile at night; zoom to the host galaxy.

### FACT #5: A monster sits at the heart of the Milky Way (6:30–7:50)

> Sabse nazdeek ka supermassive black hole, Milky Way ke theek center mein hai, lagbhag 26,000 light-years door. Naam hai... Sagittarius A-star. Uske aur Earth ke beech dhool ki moti deewarein hain, lekin infrared roshni unke aar-paar nikal jaati hai.
>
> 1990 ke dashak se, do teams ne center ke paas ghoomte taaron par nazar rakhi. Ek ki aguai Germany ke Reinhard Genzel kar rahe the, Chile ke telescopes se. Doosri ki, America ki Andrea Ghez, Hawaii ke Keck telescopes se. Teen dashak tak, saal dar saal, taaron ki chaal record hoti rahi.
>
> Ek taara sabse khaas nikla... S-two. Woh ek adrishya cheez ke charon taraf har 16 saal mein ek chakkar lagata hai. 2018 mein, sabse kareeb pahunch kar, uski raftaar dhaai crore kilometer prati ghanta se bhi zyaada thi. Itni raftaar ke liye, us adrishya cheez ka mass Sun se lagbhag 40 lakh guna hona chahiye, aur woh bhi itni chhoti jagah mein, ki black hole ke alawa koi jawab bachta hi nahi.
>
> 2020 mein, Genzel aur Ghez ko Physics ka Nobel Prize mila. Unke saath Roger Penrose ko bhi, jinhone dikhaya tha, ki black holes ka banna Einstein ki theory ka pakka nateeja hai. Andrea Ghez, Physics ka Nobel jeetne waali sirf chauthi mahila bani.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #5 · A MONSTER SITS AT THE HEART OF THE MILKY WAY` · `SAGITTARIUS A*` · `26,000 light-years` · `Reinhard Genzel` · `Andrea Ghez` · `S2` · `One orbit every 16 years` · `2018: 25 million km/h` · `4 million × the Sun's mass` · `NOBEL PRIZE 2020` · `Roger Penrose`
- **Visuals:** ESO zoom into the Galactic Centre; stars orbiting Sgr A* (ESO animations); VLT lasers; graphic: S2's ellipse around the centre.

### FACT #4: When they collide, space itself shakes (7:50–9:00)

> 14 September 2015. America mein, ek doosre se 3,000 kilometer door, do vishaal detectors ne ek hi kaanpti hui lehar pakdi. Yeh the LIGO ke detectors.
>
> Einstein ne sau saal pehle kaha tha, ki bhaari cheezein jab tezi se ghoomti hain, to space aur time khud lehron mein kaanpne lagte hain... gravitational waves. Lekin yeh lehrein itni kamzor hoti hain, ki Einstein ko shak tha, ki insaan unhe kabhi naap paayega.
>
> Yeh lehar 1 arab 30 crore saal pehle chali thi, jab Sun se 36 aur 29 guna bhaari do black holes, ek doosre ke chakkar lagate hue aapas mein samaa gaye. Aakhri pal mein, teen Suns jitna mass seedha energy ban gaya. Ek second ke chhote se hisse ke liye, is takkar ki taakat, dikhne wale brahmaand ke saare taaron ki milikar roshni se bhi zyaada thi.
>
> Earth tak pahunch kar, is lehar ne LIGO ki chaar kilometer lambi bhujaaon ko, ek proton ki chaudai ke bhi hazaarwen hisse jitna hi khincha. Phir bhi woh pakdi gayi. 2017 mein is khoj ko Nobel Prize mila. Paane waalon mein ek the Kip Thorne... wahi, jinhone Hawking se shart jeeti thi.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #4 · WHEN THEY COLLIDE, SPACE ITSELF SHAKES` · `14 SEPTEMBER 2015` · `LIGO` · `3,000 km apart` · `GRAVITATIONAL WAVES` · `1.3 billion years ago` · `36 + 29 Suns` · `3 Suns → pure energy` · `1/1000 the width of a proton` · `NOBEL PRIZE 2017` · `Kip Thorne`
- **Visuals:** binary black hole merger visualizations (NASA SVS); graphic: rippling spacetime grid; Earth from space.

### FACT #3: It took a telescope the size of Earth to see one (9:00–10:05)

> Ab tak black hole ka sirf asar dekha gaya tha, black hole nahi. Itni door ki itni chhoti cheez ki tasveer ke liye, Earth jitna bada telescope chahiye tha.
>
> Scientists ne theek wahi banaya... Event Horizon Telescope. Hawaii se Chile tak, Spain se South Pole tak, radio telescopes ne ek hi raat, ek hi disha mein dekha, jaise woh ek hi vishaal telescope hon. Data itna zyaada tha, ki internet se bhejna mumkin nahi tha. Lagbhag ek hazaar hard drives, hawaai jahaazon se laayi gayin.
>
> 10 April 2019 ko, duniya ne pehli baar ek black hole ki tasveer dekhi. Galaxy M87 ke center mein, saadhe paanch crore light-years door, Sun se saadhe chhah arab guna bhaari black hole. Chamakti gas ka ek chhalla, aur beech mein gehra andhera... black hole ki parchhaai.
>
> 2022 mein, Milky Way ke apne black hole, Sagittarius A-star ki tasveer bhi saamne aayi.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #3 · IT TOOK A TELESCOPE THE SIZE OF EARTH TO SEE ONE` · `EVENT HORIZON TELESCOPE` · `Hawaii · Chile · Spain · South Pole` · `~1,000 hard drives` · `10 APRIL 2019` · `M87*` · `55 million light-years` · `6.5 billion × the Sun's mass` · `2022: SAGITTARIUS A*`
- **Visuals:** ALMA / APEX radio dishes at night (ESO); Earth rotating; ESO zoom into M87; the M87* and Sgr A* images inside a moving graphic (EHT, CC BY 4.0).

### FACT #2: Hawking was right, 54 years later (10:05–11:10)

> 1971 mein, Stephen Hawking ne ek niyam diya. Jab do black holes milte hain, to naye black hole ki satah ka kshetrafal, purane dono ke kul kshetrafal se kabhi kam nahi ho sakta.
>
> Ise parakhne ke liye, ek takkar se pehle aur baad, dono black holes ko naapna zaroori tha. Dashkon tak yeh namumkin raha.
>
> 14 January 2025 ko, LIGO ne ab tak ki sabse saaf gravitational wave pakdi. Ek arab se zyaada light-years door, Sun se tees guna se zyaada bhaari do black holes aapas mein mil gaye. Signal 2015 se chaar guna zyaada saaf tha, itna ki takkar se pehle ki lehrein, aur baad mein naye black hole ki aakhri kaanpan, dono alag-alag naapi ja sakin.
>
> Takkar se pehle kul satah, lagbhag 2 lakh 40 hazaar square kilometer. Takkar ke baad, lagbhag 4 lakh square kilometer. 54 saal baad, Hawking ka niyam apni sabse kathin pareeksha mein khara utra. Lekin Hawking yeh dekhne ke liye zinda nahi the. Unka dehant 2018 mein ho chuka tha.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #2 · HAWKING WAS RIGHT, 54 YEARS LATER` · `1971` · `Hawking's Area Theorem` · `14 JANUARY 2025` · `GW250114` · `The clearest signal ever` · `4× clearer than 2015` · `Before: ~240,000 km²` · `After: ~400,000 km²` · `Stephen Hawking 1942–2018`
- **Visuals:** black hole merger visualizations; graphic: two horizon spheres (240,000 km²) merging into a bigger one (400,000 km²); ringdown wave.

### FACT #1: Some may be older than galaxies (11:10–12:40)

> Lambe samay tak black holes ki ek hi kahaani maani jaati thi. Pehle taare bante hain, phir galaxies. Bhaari taare marte hain, black holes bante hain, aur arabon saalon mein dheere-dheere bade hote hain.
>
> James Webb Space Telescope ne is kahaani ko hila diya. Brahmaand ke shuruaati daur mein, Webb ko chhote, laal, chamakte dhabbe dikhe... Little Red Dots. Inke andar ummeed se kahin bade black holes chhupe the.
>
> May 2026 mein, University of Cambridge ki aguai mein ek team ne, inme se ek, QSO-One, ke black hole ka mass pehli baar seedha naapa. Uski roshni tab chali thi, jab brahmaand sirf lagbhag 70 crore saal ka tha. Black hole Sun se lagbhag 5 crore guna bhaari nikla. Aur uske charon taraf galaxy lagbhag thi hi nahi. Poore system ka do-tihaai mass, akela black hole tha. Wahan ki gas lagbhag shuddh hydrogen aur helium thi, jaisi Big Bang ke turant baad thi.
>
> Scientists ke mutaabik, iska sabse seedha matlab hai, ki yeh black hole apni galaxy se pehle bana. Shayad gas ke kisi vishaal badal ke seedhe dhehne se. Ya shayad... Big Bang ke pehle hi second mein.
>
> 1915 mein, jang ke maidan mein, Karl Schwarzschild ne kaagaz par ek ajeeb seema khoji thi. Aaj insaan us seema ki tasveer le chuka hai, black holes ki takkaron ki goonj sun chuka hai, aur unhe brahmaand ki shuruaat tak dekh chuka hai. Lekin sabse bada sawaal ab bhi wahin khada hai: event horizon ke us paar kya hai? Uske bilkul center mein, Einstein ke niyam bhi jawab de dete hain. Aur jis din insaan us andhere ke andar jhaank paayega, shayad us din woh yeh bhi samajh paayega, ki yeh brahmaand khud kahaan se aaya.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #1 · SOME MAY BE OLDER THAN GALAXIES` · `JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE` · `"LITTLE RED DOTS"` · `MAY 2026` · `QSO1` · `13+ billion years old light` · `~700 million years after the Big Bang` · `50 million × the Sun's mass` · `2/3 of all the mass: the black hole` · `Almost pure hydrogen and helium` · `Primordial black holes?` · final `1915 → 2026` · final card `What lies beyond the event horizon?`
- **Visuals:** Webb in space; JWST deep fields; Little Red Dots / early-universe black hole animations (ESA/Webb); Big Bang visualization; final slow push into a black hole visualization, ending on the final card.
- **Sound:** the biggest boom lands on "Big Bang ke pehle hi second mein". The drone fades out over about 3 s after the last word, then a hard stop. No subscribe ask.
