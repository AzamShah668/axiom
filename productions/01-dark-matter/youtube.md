10 Facts You Don't Know About Dark Matter | Hindi Documentary

Every star, planet and galaxy ever observed makes up only about 15% of the matter in the universe. The rest is dark matter: invisible, untouchable, and holding every galaxy together.

This Hindi documentary follows the story of dark matter. It begins with Fritz Zwicky's forgotten discovery in 1933 and Vera Rubin's impossibly fast galaxies. It then visits the giant invisible halo around the Milky Way, Einstein's gravitational lensing, and the Bullet Cluster collision. It shows how dark matter built the cosmic web, and the "dark matter wind" flowing through Earth right now.

It ends with the biggest open question in physics. What is dark matter made of? And did the LZ detector, 1.5 km underground, catch its first hint in 2026?
