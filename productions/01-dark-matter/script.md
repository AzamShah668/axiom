# Production 01: 10 Facts You Don't Know About Dark Matter | Hindi Documentary

Channel rules (packaging, narration style, edit method): see [`../README.md`](../README.md).

- The narration is **third-person storytelling**: one continuous story from 1933 to 2026. It never addresses the viewer and never announces numbers.
- The "10 facts" countdown lives **only on screen**, as English chapter cards (`FACT #10 …`).

## Script

The narration is about 1,700 words. Real timing comes from the first ElevenLabs render (target about 11–12 min), and section times below are estimates. **Every number spoken appears on screen in English within about 1 second**, with a soft "pop" sound effect. Each chapter card gets a whoosh.

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:45)

> Saal 1933. California Institute of Technology mein, Swiss astronomer Fritz Zwicky ek ajeeb hisaab mein uljhe hue the. Lagbhag 30 crore light-years door, hazaar se zyaada galaxies ka ek vishaal jhund tha: Coma Cluster. Zwicky ne in galaxies ki raftaar ka hisaab lagaya, aur kuch gadbad nikli.
>
> Galaxies itni tezi se bhaag rahi thin, ki dikhne wale saare taaron ki gravity milkar bhi unhe baandh kar nahi rakh sakti thi. Cluster ko kab ka bikhar jaana chahiye tha. Phir bhi woh tika hua tha, jaise koi adrishya haath use thaame hue ho.
>
> Zwicky ne us anjaan cheez ko ek naam diya: "dunkle Materie". Dark Matter.
>
> Lagbhag sau saal baad bhi, yeh rahasya suljha nahi hai. Aur 2026 mein, zameen se dedh kilometer neeche, ek detector ne ek aisi takkar record ki, jise ab tak koi samjha nahi paaya.

- **On-screen:** `1933` · `Fritz Zwicky` · `Coma Cluster` · `~300 million light-years away` · `1,000+ galaxies` · `"Dunkle Materie"` → title card `10 FACTS ABOUT DARK MATTER`

### FACT #10: Discovered in 1933, then ignored for 40 years (0:45–1:30)

> Zwicky ke hisaab se, Coma Cluster ko thaamne ke liye dikhne wale matter se sau guna se bhi zyaada mass chahiye tha. Yeh sankhya itni ajeeb thi, ki zyaadatar astronomers ne ise naap-tol ki galti maan kar nazarandaaz kar diya. Zwicky apne tez mizaaj ke liye bhi mashhoor the, aur unki baaton ko aksar gambheerta se nahi liya jaata tha.
>
> Dheere-dheere dunkle Materie ka vichaar ek purane research paper ke panno mein dab kar reh gaya. Coma Cluster apni jagah chupchaap ghoomta raha, aur uske saath woh sawaal bhi, jiska jawab kisi ke paas nahi tha. Agle lagbhag 40 saalon tak, duniya is chhupe hue mass ko lagbhag bhool chuki thi.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #10 · DISCOVERED IN 1933, IGNORED FOR 40 YEARS` · `100× more mass needed`
- **Visuals:**
  - Zwicky portrait (only if its licence is clear; otherwise a silhouette card).
  - Archival-style paper texture with the year.
  - A slow drift across the Coma Cluster as time-lapse years tick on screen, `1933 → 1970`.

### FACT #9: A woman proved it, and never got a Nobel Prize (1:30–2:45)

> 1970 ke dashak mein, yeh bhoola hua sawaal phir se zinda hua, is baar American astronomer Vera Rubin ke haathon. Rubin aur unke saathi Kent Ford galaxies ke ghoomne ki raftaar naap rahe the, shuruaat padosi galaxy Andromeda se.
>
> Solar system mein niyam seedha hai. Sun se jitna door grah, utni dheemi raftaar. Earth lagbhag 30 kilometer prati second se chakkar lagati hai, jabki door Neptune sirf 5 kilometer prati second se. Galaxies mein bhi yahi hona chahiye tha: center ke paas taare tez, aur kinare par dheeme.
>
> Lekin Rubin ke data ne kuch aur hi kahaani sunayi. Galaxy ke bilkul kinare par maujood taare bhi utni hi tezi se ghoom rahe the, jitne center ke paas wale. Itni raftaar par unhe galaxy se bahar chhitak jaana chahiye tha. Phir bhi galaxy salamat thi. Ek galaxy ke baad doosri, har jagah wahi nateeja.
>
> Iska ek hi matlab tha. Har galaxy ke andar aur uske charon taraf, koi bhaari, adrishya cheez maujood thi. Zwicky sahi the.
>
> Vera Rubin ka 2016 mein dehant ho gaya, aur unhe kabhi Nobel Prize nahi mila. Aaj Chile ke pahadon par khadi, duniya ki sabse aadhunik observatories mein se ek, unke naam par hai: Vera C. Rubin Observatory.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #9 · THE WOMAN WHO PROVED IT` · `Vera Rubin (1928–2016)` · `Kent Ford` · `Andromeda` · `Earth: 30 km/s` · `Neptune: 5 km/s` · graph lines `Expected` vs `Observed` · `Vera C. Rubin Observatory, Chile`
- **Visuals:**

  - **Code infographic:** the rotation curve. The "Expected" line falls away while the "Observed" line stays flat, drawn in sync with the narration.
  - Rubin portrait + Rubin Observatory photos (NOIRLab/NSF/AURA, CC BY 4.0).

### FACT #8: Every galaxy hides inside a giant invisible sphere (2:45–3:40)

> Rubin ki khoj ke baad, galaxies ki tasveer hamesha ke liye badal gayi. Milky Way lagbhag 1 lakh light-years chaudi hai, aur usme 100 arab se zyaada taare hain. Lekin yeh chamakti hui disk, poori galaxy ka sirf andar ka hissa nikli.
>
> Iske charon taraf phaila hai ek vishaal, adrishya gola, jise dark matter halo kehte hain. Yeh halo dikhne wali galaxy se 10 guna se bhi zyaada chauda hai. Anumaan hai ki Milky Way ke kul mass ka lagbhag 90 percent isi halo mein hai. Arabon taaron se bani yeh galaxy, asal mein ek adrishya samundar ke beech tairti, roshni ki ek chhoti si tikiya hai.
>
> Aur yeh akeli Milky Way ki kahaani nahi. Brahmand ki lagbhag har badi galaxy, aise hi ek dark matter halo ke andar paida hui, aur usi ke andar pali-badhi.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #8 · EVERY GALAXY HAS A HIDDEN HALO` · `The Milky Way` · `100,000 light-years` · `100+ billion stars` · `Dark Matter Halo` · `10× wider` · `~90% of the Milky Way's mass`
- **Visuals:**
  - SpaceEngine flight out to view the Milky Way disk from outside.
  - **Our overlay:** the purple halo sphere expands to 10× the disk, with a growing scale bar.
  - A grid of other galaxies, each getting a halo.

### FACT #7: Invisible to every telescope ever built (3:40–4:30)

> Itna vishaal, phir bhi poori tarah ojhal. Hubble, James Webb, radio dishes, X-ray telescopes: insaan ke banaye yeh yantra roshni ki har wavelength mein brahmand ko dekh sakte hain, radio waves se lekar gamma rays tak. Lekin dark matter in mein se kisi ko dikhai nahi deta. Woh roshni ke saath koi rishta rakhta hi nahi. Na chamakta hai, na roshni sokhta hai, na use lautaata hai.
>
> Uski maujoodgi ka sirf ek saboot hai: gravity. Bilkul hawa ki tarah, jo khud kabhi dikhai nahi deti, lekin pedon ki hilti shaakhein uski kahaani keh deti hain. Dark matter ki kahaani bhi, galaxies ki chaal aur taaron ki raftaar likhti hai.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #7 · INVISIBLE AT EVERY WAVELENGTH` · `Radio → Gamma Rays` · `Detected only through GRAVITY`
- **Visuals:**
  - Telescope montage: Hubble, JWST, Chandra (NASA), ALMA (ESO, CC BY), VLA radio dishes.
  - **Code infographic:** a scanner sweeps the spectrum from radio to gamma; the galaxy shows up at every band, but the halo never does.
  - Wind-in-trees stock clip (Pexels, free).

### FACT #6: It bends light (4:30–5:30)

> Lekin gravity ek aur nishaan chhodti hai. 1915 mein Albert Einstein ne dikhaya tha ki bhaari cheezein space aur time ko mod deti hain, aur us mude hue raaste par chalti roshni bhi mud jaati hai. Ise kehte hain gravitational lensing.
>
> Jab kisi door ki galaxy ki roshni, beech mein maujood dark matter ke paas se guzarti hai, to woh mud jaati hai. Galaxy khinchi hui, tedhi, ya kabhi-kabhi ek chamakte chhalle jaisi dikhne lagti hai. In tedhi-medhi tasveeron ko ulta padh kar, astronomers ne yeh naksha banana seekha, ki dark matter kahaan hai, aur kitna hai.
>
> 2007 mein, Hubble Space Telescope ki tasveeron se, lagbhag 5 arab light-years door ek galaxy cluster ke beech, dark matter ka ek vishaal chhalla saamne aaya. Pehli baar, ek adrishya cheez ka naksha kaagaz par utra tha.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #6 · IT BENDS LIGHT` · `Gravitational Lensing` · `Einstein, 1915` · `Hubble, 2007` · `Dark Matter Ring` · `Galaxy cluster CL0024+17` · `~5 billion light-years away`
- **Visuals:**
  - **Code/Blender:** a space-time grid bending under a mass, with a light ray curving around it.
  - Lensing arcs: JWST SMACS 0723 (NASA/ESA/CSA/STScI) and a Hubble Einstein ring.
  - The CL0024+17 dark matter ring image (NASA/ESA/M.J. Jee & H. Ford) with a slow push-in.

### FACT #5: A cosmic crash ripped it apart from normal matter (5:30–6:40)

> Phir bhi, kuch scientists ko shaq tha. Kya pata dark matter jaisi koi cheez ho hi na, aur gravity ke niyam hi bade paimaane par badal jaate hon? Is bahas ka sabse bada jawab, ek brahmandiya takkar se aaya.
>
> Arabon light-years door, do vishaal galaxy clusters aapas mein takraaye. Takkar ke baad, chhota cluster lagbhag 4,700 kilometer prati second ki raftaar se aage nikal raha hai. Is jodi ko naam mila: Bullet Cluster.
>
> Kisi bhi cluster ka zyaadatar normal matter, behad garam gas ke roop mein hota hai, itni garam ki woh X-rays mein chamakti hai. Takkar mein yeh gas aapas mein ulajh kar dheemi padi, aur beech mein hi atak gayi. Lekin jab gravitational lensing se asal mass ka naksha banaya gaya, to zyaadatar mass gas ke saath tha hi nahi. Woh bina ruke, ek-doosre ke aar-paar nikal chuka tha.
>
> 2006 mein jab yeh tasveer saamne aayi, to ise dark matter ke hone ka ab tak ka sabse seedha saboot maana gaya.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #5 · THE COLLISION THAT REVEALED IT` · `Bullet Cluster` · `~4,700 km/s` · `Hot gas (X-ray)` in pink · `Mass (lensing)` in blue · `2006: "Direct proof"`
- **Visuals:**
  - The NASA Chandra **Bullet Cluster composite** (pink gas, blue mass) with labels.
  - Chandra's collision animation (NASA/CXC, credit).
  - **Code infographic:** two blobs collide. Pink gas stops in the middle while the blue halos pass through.
- **Sound:** a deep impact/whoosh at the collision moment. This is the second-biggest SFX in the video.

### FACT #4: There is 5 times more of it than normal matter (6:40–7:45)

> Ab sawaal yeh tha, ki yeh adrishya cheez aakhir kitni hai. Iska jawab Big Bang ki bachi hui roshni, yaani cosmic microwave background, mein chhupa tha. 2013 se 2018 ke beech, European Space Agency ke Planck satellite ne is roshni ko behad baareeki se naapa.
>
> Nateeja chaunka dene wala tha. Brahmand ke kul matter ka lagbhag 85 percent, dark matter hai. Har 1 kilo normal matter ke saath, lagbhag 5 kilo dark matter. Taare, grah, gas, dhool, aur har jaandaar cheez, sab milkar sirf 15 percent.
>
> Aur jab brahmand ki poori energy bhi hisaab mein judi, to normal matter ka hissa sirf 5 percent reh gaya. Dark matter lagbhag 27 percent. Aur baaki 68 percent: dark energy, ek bilkul alag rahasya, jo brahmand ko aur tezi se phaila rahi hai. Dark matter cheezon ko jodta hai, dark energy unhe door dhakelti hai.
>
> Insaan ne jis brahmand ko sadiyon tak dekha aur naapa, woh poori tasveer ka sirf 5 percent nikla.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #4 · 5× MORE THAN NORMAL MATTER` · `Planck Satellite (ESA)` · `Dark Matter ≈ 85% of all matter` · `1 kg : 5 kg` · pie chart `Normal Matter 5%` / `Dark Matter 27%` / `Dark Energy 68%`
- **Visuals:**
  - Planck CMB map (ESA and the Planck Collaboration) on a slowly rotating sphere.
  - **Code infographic:** a balance scale tips from 1 kg to 5 kg.
  - **Code infographic:** an animated pie chart.
  - SpaceEngine shot of galaxies receding on "dark energy".

### FACT #3: Without it, galaxies would never have formed (7:45–8:50)

> Lekin dark matter sirf galaxies ko thaamta nahi. Usi ne unhe banaya bhi.
>
> Big Bang ke baad lagbhag 3 lakh 80 hazaar saal tak, normal matter roshni ke saath itna uljha hua tha, ki woh kahin jama hi nahi ho sakta tha. Dark matter par roshni ka koi asar nahi tha. Isliye woh pehle hi gravity ke khinchav se simatne laga, aur poore brahmand mein dhaagon aur gaanthon ka ek vishaal jaal bun gaya. Ise kehte hain, cosmic web.
>
> Jab normal matter aazaad hua, to woh dark matter ke in gaddhon mein gir pada. Wahin gas jama hui, wahin pehle taare jale, wahin pehli galaxies bani. James Webb Telescope ne aisi galaxies bhi dhoondhi hain, jo Big Bang ke sirf lagbhag 30 crore saal baad chamak rahi thin.
>
> Dark matter ke bina, galaxies ko banne mein kahin zyaada samay lagta. Shayad aaj tak na Milky Way hoti, na Sun, na Earth... aur na hi zindagi.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #3 · IT BUILT THE UNIVERSE` · `380,000 years after the Big Bang` · `The Cosmic Web` · `JWST` · `~300 million years after the Big Bang`
- **Visuals:**
  - Cosmic-web simulation fly-through (ESA Euclid animations, CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO, or IllustrisTNG with credit; licence to be confirmed).
  - JWST early-galaxy imagery (JADES, NASA/ESA/CSA).
  - SpaceEngine zoom from the cosmic web to the Milky Way, then the Sun, then Earth.

### FACT #2: It is flowing through Earth right now (8:50–9:45)

> Aur yeh adrishya jaal kahin door nahi hai. Woh yahin hai.
>
> Sun, Milky Way ke center ke charon taraf lagbhag 230 kilometer prati second ki raftaar se chakkar laga raha hai, aur uske saath poora solar system. Lekin dark matter halo is tarah nahi ghoomta. Isliye galaxy mein aage badhte hue, Earth lagataar ek "dark matter ki hawa" ke beech se guzar rahi hai. Yeh hawa isi pal deewaron, pahadon, samundaron, aur har insaan ke shareer ke aar-paar beh rahi hai, bina kisi cheez se takraaye, bina koi nishaan chhode.
>
> Earth ke aas-paas dark matter itna patla phaila hai, ki Earth jitne bade gole mein maujood saara dark matter, milkar 1 kilo se bhi kam hoga. Lekin jab yahi patla sa dark matter poori galaxy mein jud jaata hai, to uska wazan lagbhag 1 trillion Suns ke barabar ho jaata hai.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #2 · IT'S FLOWING THROUGH EARTH RIGHT NOW` · `230 km/s` · `"Dark Matter Wind"` · `Earth-sized volume of dark matter: < 1 kg` · `Milky Way's dark matter: ~1 trillion Suns`
- **Visuals:**
  - **Code infographic:** a top-down view of the Sun orbiting the galactic centre.
  - **Code particle animation:** a stream of faint particles passes straight through Earth, then through mountains, the sea and a human silhouette.
  - **Code infographic:** an Earth-sized sphere shrinks to a 1 kg icon, then pulls back to the whole halo.

### FACT #1: Nobody knows what it is (9:45–11:15)

> Lagbhag sau saal ki khoj ke baad bhi, sabse bada sawaal wahin khada hai: dark matter asal mein hai kya?
>
> Use pakadne ki koshish lagataar chal rahi hai. America ke South Dakota mein, zameen se lagbhag dedh kilometer neeche, LZ naam ka ek detector hai, jisme lagbhag 10 ton liquid xenon bhara hai. Itni gehrai mein, upar se aane wali radiation us tak nahi pahunch paati. Agar dark matter ka ek bhi kan xenon ke kisi atom se takraaye, to roshni ki ek halki si chamak use pakad sakti hai.
>
> 1 September 2026 ko, LZ ki team ne ek ajeeb nateeja saamne rakha. 220 din ke data mein sirf ek takkar, jise koi bhi jaani-pehchaani cheez samjha nahi paa rahi. Iske sirf ittefaq hone ki sambhavna lagbhag 200 mein 1 hai. Lekin physics mein kisi khoj ko pakka maanne ke liye, yeh sambhavna lagbhag 35 lakh mein 1 honi chahiye. Team ne saaf kaha, ki woh dark matter dekhne ka daawa nahi kar rahi.
>
> Agar yeh dark matter nahi nikla, to candidates ki list abhi bhi lambi hai. WIMPs naam ke bhaari kan. Axions, jo electron se arabon guna halke ho sakte hain. Big Bang ke samay bane chhote black holes. Aur kuch scientists ka maanna hai ki shayad dark matter hai hi nahi, aur gravity ke niyam hi galaxies ke paimaane par adhoore hain.
>
> Brahmand ka 85 percent matter har taraf maujood hai, har galaxy ko thaame hue hai, har insaan ke aar-paar guzar raha hai... aur ab tak koi nahi jaanta ki woh hai kya. Shayad brahmand ki sabse badi khoj abhi baaki hai. Aur jis din dark matter ka rahasya khulega, us din insaan shayad yeh bhi samajh paayega, ki is vishaal brahmand mein uski asal jagah kahaan hai.

- **On-screen:** card `FACT #1 · NOBODY KNOWS WHAT IT IS` · `LZ Detector` · `~1.5 km underground` · `~10 tonnes of liquid xenon` · `1 September 2026: 1 unexplained event` · `Fluke chance: ~1 in 200` · `Discovery needs: ~1 in 3.5 million` · `"We are not claiming to have seen dark matter."` · `WIMPs • Axions • Primordial Black Holes` · `Modified Gravity?` · final card `85% of all matter. Still unknown.`
- **Visuals:**
