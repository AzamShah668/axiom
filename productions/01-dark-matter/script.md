### #10 — It is completely invisible (0:40–1:35)

> Number 10. Dark matter poori tarah invisible hai.
>
> Hamare paas aaj duniya ke sabse taakatwar telescopes hain: Hubble, James Webb, radio telescopes, X-ray telescopes. Yeh roshni ki har wavelength mein dekh sakte hain, radio waves se lekar gamma rays tak. Lekin dark matter in mein se kisi ko bhi dikhai nahi deta, kyunki woh roshni ke saath koi interaction karta hi nahi. Na woh chamakta hai, na garam hota hai, na kisi cheez ki parchhai banata hai.
>
> To phir humein pata kaise chala ki woh hai? Sirf ek tareeke se: gravity. Dark matter dikhta nahi, lekin uska khinchav poore brahmand mein mehsoos hota hai. Bilkul hawa ki tarah. Hawa dikhai nahi deti, lekin hilte hue pedon ko dekh kar hum jaan jaate hain ki woh chal rahi hai.
>
> Brahmand ki sabse zyaada maujood cheez, aur hum use sirf uske asar se pehchante hain.

- **On-screen:** number card `#10` · `Radio → Gamma Rays` · `Invisible at every wavelength` · `Detected only through GRAVITY`
- **Visuals:**

### #9 — There is 5 times more of it than normal matter (1:35–2:35)

> Number 9. Brahmand ka zyaadatar matter, dark matter hai.
>
> Agar brahmand ke saare matter ko ek tarazu par tola jaaye, to har 1 kilo normal matter ke saath lagbhag 5 kilo dark matter hoga. Yaani kul matter ka lagbhag 85 percent dark matter hai. Taare, grah, gas, dhool, aap aur main, sab milkar sirf 15 percent.
>
> Aur kahaani yahin khatam nahi hoti. Agar brahmand ki poori energy aur matter ko ek saath dekhein, to normal matter sirf 5 percent hai. Dark matter lagbhag 27 percent. Aur baaki 68 percent? Woh hai dark energy, ek bilkul alag rahasya. Naam milte-julte hain, lekin kaam ulta hai. Dark matter cheezon ko apni taraf kheenchta hai, aur dark energy brahmand ko aur tezi se phaila rahi hai.
>
> Yaani jis brahmand ko hum dekh aur samajh paate hain, woh poori kahaani ka sirf 5 percent hai.

### #8 — It was discovered in 1933, then ignored for 40 years (2:35–3:35)

> Number 8. Dark matter ka pehla suraag 90 saal se bhi pehle mila tha.
>
> Saal 1933. Swiss astronomer Fritz Zwicky, Coma Cluster ka adhyayan kar rahe the. Yeh hazaar se zyaada galaxies ka ek vishaal jhund hai, jo humse lagbhag 30 crore light-years door hai. Zwicky ne naapa ki is cluster ki galaxies kitni tezi se chal rahi hain. Aur nateeja chaunka dene wala tha. Galaxies itni tez thin ki dikhne wale matter ki gravity unhe kabhi baandh kar nahi rakh sakti thi. Cluster ko kab ka bikhar jaana chahiye tha.
>
> Zwicky ke hisaab se, is cluster ko thaamne ke liye dikhne wale matter se sau guna se bhi zyaada mass chahiye tha. Unhone is chhupe hue mass ko naam diya: "dunkle Materie", yaani dark matter.
>
> Lekin us waqt kisi ne unki baat ko gambheerta se nahi liya. Agle lagbhag 40 saalon tak, dark matter ek bhooli hui theory bana raha.

- **On-screen:** `#8` · `1933` · `Fritz Zwicky` · `Coma Cluster` · `1,000+ galaxies` · `~300 million light-years away` · `"Dunkle Materie" = Dark Matter` · `100× more mass needed`
- **Visuals:**

  - An old-paper styled date card `1933`.

  - **Code infographic:** galaxies flying outward ("should fly apart") vs. held together.
- **Sound:** a soft typewriter tick on the `1933` card.

### #7 — A woman proved it, and never got a Nobel Prize (3:35–4:45)

> Number 7. Dark matter ko sabit karne wali scientist ko kabhi Nobel Prize nahi mila.
>
> 1970 ke dashak mein, American astronomer Vera Rubin galaxies ke ghoomne ki speed naap rahi thin. Hamare solar system mein, Sun se door wale grah dheere chalte hain. Earth lagbhag 30 kilometer prati second se ghoomti hai, lekin Neptune sirf 5 kilometer prati second se. Galaxies mein bhi yahi hona chahiye tha: center ke paas wale taare tez, aur kinare wale dheere.
>
> Lekin Vera Rubin ne jo dekha, usne sab kuch badal diya. Galaxy ke bilkul kinare par maujood taare bhi utni hi tezi se ghoom rahe the, jitne center ke paas wale. Itni speed par, in taaron ko galaxy se bahar uchhal jaana chahiye tha. Phir bhi galaxies salamat thin.
>
> Iska ek hi matlab tha. Har galaxy ke andar aur uske charon taraf, koi bahut bhaari, adrishya cheez hai, jo un taaron ko apni gravity se thaame hue hai.
>
> Vera Rubin ka 2016 mein dehant ho gaya, aur unhe kabhi Nobel nahi mila. Lekin aaj Chile ke pahadon mein, duniya ki sabse aadhunik observatories mein se ek, unke naam par hai: Vera C. Rubin Observatory.

- **On-screen:** `#7` · `Vera Rubin (1928–2016)` · `Earth: 30 km/s` · `Neptune: 5 km/s` · graph lines `Expected` vs `Observed` · `Vera C. Rubin Observatory, Chile`

### #6 — The Milky Way lives inside a giant invisible sphere (4:45–5:45)

> Number 6. Hamari Milky Way, dark matter ke ek vishaal gole ke andar band hai.
>
> Milky Way lagbhag 1 lakh light-years chaudi hai, aur isme 100 arab se zyaada taare hain. Lekin yeh chamakti hui disk sirf andar ka hissa hai. Iske charon taraf phaila hai ek vishaal, adrishya gola, jise dark matter halo kehte hain. Aur yeh halo, dikhne wali galaxy se 10 guna se bhi zyaada chauda hai.
>
> Scientists ka anumaan hai ki Milky Way ke kul mass ka lagbhag 90 percent isi halo mein hai. Matlab arabon taaron se bani hamari galaxy, asal mein ek adrishya samundar ke beech tairti hui roshni ki ek chhoti si tikiya hai.
>
> Aur yeh sirf hamari kahaani nahi. Brahmand ki lagbhag har badi galaxy, ek aise hi dark matter halo ke andar paida hui, aur usi ke andar badi hui.

- **On-screen:** `#6` · `The Milky Way` · `100,000 light-years` · `100+ billion stars` · `Dark Matter Halo` · `10× wider` · `~90% of the Milky Way's mass`

### #5 — Dark matter is passing through you right now (5:45–6:50)

> Number 5. Dark matter isi pal, aapke shareer ke aar-paar guzar raha hai.
>
> Hamara Sun, galaxy ke center ke charon taraf lagbhag 230 kilometer prati second ki raftaar se chakkar laga raha hai, aur Earth ke saath hum sab bhi. Lekin dark matter halo is tarah nahi ghoomta. Isliye galaxy mein aage badhte hue, hum lagataar ek "dark matter ki hawa" se hokar guzar rahe hain. Yeh hawa isi waqt aapke kamre, aapki deewaron aur aapke shareer ke aar-paar beh rahi hai. Aapko kuch mehsoos nahi hota, kyunki dark matter kisi cheez se takrata hi nahi.
>
> Aur yahan ek aur hairaan kar dene wali baat hai. Hamare aas-paas dark matter itna patla phaila hua hai, ki Earth jitne bade gole mein maujood saara dark matter, milkar 1 kilo se bhi kam wazan ka hoga.
>
> Lekin jab yahi patla sa dark matter poori galaxy mein jud jaata hai, to uska wazan lagbhag 1 trillion Suns ke barabar ho jaata hai.

- **On-screen:** `#5` · `230 km/s` · `"Dark Matter Wind"` · `Earth-sized volume of dark matter: < 1 kg` · `Milky Way's dark matter: ~1 trillion Suns`

### #4 — It is invisible, but it bends light (6:50–7:50)

> Number 4. Dark matter dikhai nahi deta, lekin roshni ko mod deta hai.
>
> 1915 mein Albert Einstein ne bataya tha ki bhaari cheezein space aur time ko mod deti hain, aur us mude hue raaste par chalne wali roshni bhi mud jaati hai. Ise kehte hain gravitational lensing. Jab kisi door ki galaxy ki roshni, beech mein maujood dark matter ke paas se guzarti hai, to woh mud jaati hai. Aur hamein woh galaxy khinchi hui, tedhi, ya kabhi-kabhi ek chamakte chhalle jaisi dikhti hai.
>
> Scientists in tedhi-medhi tasveeron ko ulta padh kar naksha bana lete hain ki dark matter kahaan hai, aur kitna hai. 2007 mein Hubble Space Telescope ne isi tareeke se, humse lagbhag 5 arab light-years door ek galaxy cluster mein, dark matter ka ek vishaal chhalla dhoondha. Insaan ne pehli baar ek adrishya cheez ki tasveer banayi thi.

- **On-screen:** `#4` · `Gravitational Lensing` · `Einstein, 1915` · `Hubble, 2007` · `Dark Matter Ring` · `Galaxy cluster CL0024+17` · `~5 billion light-years away`
- **Visuals:**
  - **Code/Blender:** a space-time grid bending under a mass, with a light ray curving around it.
  - Lensing arcs: JWST's first deep field SMACS 0723 (NASA/ESA/CSA/STScI) and a Hubble Einstein ring.
  - The CL0024+17 dark matter ring image (NASA/ESA/M.J. Jee & H. Ford) with a slow push-in.

### #3 — A cosmic crash pulled dark matter apart from normal matter (7:50–8:55)

> Number 3. Do galaxy clusters ki takkar ne, dark matter ko normal matter se alag kar diya.
>
> Arabon light-years door, do vishaal galaxy clusters aapas mein takraaye. Is takkar ke baad chhota cluster lagbhag 4,700 kilometer prati second ki raftaar se aage nikal raha hai. Ise kehte hain, Bullet Cluster.
>
> Kisi cluster ka zyaadatar normal matter, garam gas ke roop mein hota hai, itni garam ki woh X-rays mein chamakti hai. Takkar ke dauraan yeh gas aapas mein ulajh kar dheemi pad gayi, aur beech mein hi atak gayi.
>
> Lekin jab scientists ne gravitational lensing se naapa ki asal mass kahaan hai, to nateeja hairaan karne wala tha. Zyaadatar mass gas ke saath nahi tha. Woh bina ruke ek-doosre ke aar-paar nikal chuka tha. Yaani clusters ka zyaadatar mass ek aisi cheez thi, jo na gas se takraati hai, na khud se.
>
> 2006 mein jab yeh tasveer saamne aayi, to ise dark matter ke hone ka ab tak ka sabse seedha saboot maana gaya.

- **On-screen:** `#3` · `Bullet Cluster` · `~4,700 km/s` · `Hot gas (X-ray)` in pink · `Mass (lensing)` in blue · `2006: "Direct proof"`
- **Visuals:**
  - The NASA Chandra **Bullet Cluster composite** (pink gas, blue mass) with labels.
  - Chandra's collision animation (NASA/CXC, credit).
  - **Code infographic:** two blobs collide. Pink gas stops in the middle while the blue halos pass through.
- **Sound:** a deep impact/whoosh at the moment of collision. This is the second-biggest SFX in the video.

### #2 — Without dark matter, you would not exist (8:55–9:55)

> Number 2. Agar dark matter na hota, to shayad aap bhi na hote.
>
> Big Bang ke baad lagbhag 3 lakh 80 hazaar saal tak, normal matter roshni ke saath itna uljha hua tha ki woh ek jagah jama hi nahi ho sakta tha. Lekin dark matter par roshni ka koi asar nahi hota. Isliye woh pehle hi gravity se jhundon mein simatne laga, aur poore brahmand mein dhaagon aur gaanthon ka ek vishaal jaal bun gaya. Ise kehte hain, cosmic web.
>
> Baad mein jab normal matter aazaad hua, to woh dark matter ke in gaddhon mein gir pada. Wahin gas jama hui, wahin pehle taare jale, wahin pehli galaxies bani. James Webb Telescope ne aisi galaxies bhi dekhi hain, jo Big Bang ke sirf lagbhag 30 crore saal baad chamak rahi thin.
>
> Dark matter ke bina, galaxies ko banne mein itna lamba samay lagta, ki shayad aaj tak na Milky Way hoti, na Sun, na Earth... aur na hi hum.

- **On-screen:** `#2` · `380,000 years after the Big Bang` · `The Cosmic Web` · `JWST` · `~300 million years after the Big Bang`
- **Visuals:**
  - Cosmic-web simulation fly-through (ESA Euclid animations, CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO, or IllustrisTNG with credit; licence to be confirmed).
  - JWST early-galaxy imagery (JADES, NASA/ESA/CSA).
  - SpaceEngine zoom from the cosmic web down to the Milky Way, then the Sun, then Earth.

### #1 — Nobody knows what it is... yet (9:55–11:05)

> Aur Number 1. Itna sab jaanne ke baad bhi, koi nahi jaanta ki dark matter asal mein hai kya.
>
> Use pakadne ki koshish lagataar chal rahi hai. America ke South Dakota mein, zameen se lagbhag 1.5 kilometer neeche, LZ naam ka ek detector hai, jisme lagbhag 10 ton liquid xenon bhara hai. Ise itni gehrai mein isliye rakha gaya hai, taaki upar se aane wali har radiation se bacha ja sake. Aur agar dark matter ka ek bhi kan, xenon ke kisi atom se takraaye, to use pakda ja sake.
>
> September 2026 mein, LZ ki team ne ek ajeeb nateeja saamne rakha. 220 din ke data mein, sirf ek takkar, jise koi bhi jaani-pehchaani cheez samjha nahi paa rahi. Iske sirf ittefaq hone ki sambhavna lagbhag 200 mein 1 hai. Lekin physics mein kisi khoj ko pakka maanne ke liye, yeh sambhavna lagbhag 35 lakh mein 1 honi chahiye. Isliye scientists ne saaf kaha hai: "Hum yeh daawa nahi kar rahe ki humne dark matter dekh liya hai."
>
> Aur agar yeh dark matter nahi nikla, to candidates ki list abhi bhi lambi hai. WIMPs naam ke bhaari kan. Axions, jo electron se arabon guna halke ho sakte hain. Ya phir Big Bang ke samay bane chhote-chhote black holes. Kuch scientists to yeh bhi maante hain ki shayad dark matter hai hi nahi, aur galaxies ke paimaane par, gravity ke hamare niyam hi adhoore hain.
>
> Ek pal ke liye sochiye. Brahmand ka 85 percent matter hamare charon taraf hai, hamare andar se guzar raha hai, har galaxy ko thaame hue hai... aur hum abhi tak nahi jaante ki woh hai kya. Shayad brahmand ki sabse badi khoj abhi baaki hai. Aur jis din hum dark matter ko samajh lenge, us din shayad hum yeh bhi samajh paayenge, ki is vishaal brahmand mein hum asal mein kahaan khade hain.
