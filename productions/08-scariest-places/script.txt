OPENING
Hamari galaxy mein lagbhag 10 crore black holes ho sakte hain, aur unme se zyaadatar ko aaj tak kisi ne dekha bhi nahi hai. Woh na chamakte hain, na koi roshni chhodte hain, bas andhere mein chupchaap ghoomte rehte hain aur kisi ko pata bhi nahi chalta. 2022 mein pehli baar scientists ne aise hi ek akele black hole ko pakda, jo Earth se sirf 5,000 light years door bhatak raha tha. Aur yeh to brahmand ki sabse daravni jagahon mein se bas pehli jagah hai. Aakhri jagah tak pahunchte pahunchte sawaal yeh nahi rahega ki brahmand kitna bada hai, balki yeh ki insaan usme se kitna kam dekh pata hai.

CHAPTER: THE INVISIBLE HUNTER
Ek black hole ko dekhna lagbhag namumkin hai, kyunki woh roshni chhodta nahi, ulta roshni ko bhi nigal jaata hai. To phir scientists ne use pakda kaise? 2011 mein door ka ek taara achanak zyaada chamakne laga, lagbhag 270 din tak chamakta raha, aur phir dheere dheere wapas normal ho gaya. Hua yeh tha ki koi cheez uske saamne se guzri thi aur apni gravity se uski roshni ko mod diya tha. Bilkul waise hi jaise paani se bhara glass apne peeche rakhi cheez ko thoda bada aur tedha dikhane lagta hai. Hubble telescope ne agle chhe saal tak us jagah par nazar rakhi, aur jab hisaab lagaya gaya to baat bilkul saaf ho gayi. Wahan ek aisi cheez thi jo hamare Suraj se kai guna bhaari thi, lekin poori tarah andheri thi. Uske saath na koi taara tha, na woh kisi galaxy ke centre mein tha, woh bas ek akela black hole tha jo antariksh mein bhatak raha hai. Achhi khabar yeh hai ki yeh Earth ki taraf nahi aa raha. Lekin buri khabar yeh hai ki aise black holes ko pehle se dekh paana lagbhag namumkin hai. Agar kabhi koi hamare solar system ke paas aaya, to shayad pehla ishaara planets ki badalti hui chaal hi hogi. Black hole kam se kam chup to rehta hai, lekin agli jagah par khatra khud roshni hai.

CHAPTER: THE MAGNETAR
December 2004 ki ek raat, antariksh mein ghoom rahe kai satellites ke instruments achanak ek saath bekaar ho gaye. Unse itni tez radiation takraayi thi ki unke detectors bhar gaye, aur Earth ki upar wali hawa bhi kuch pal ke liye hil gayi. Aur yeh radiation aayi thi 50,000 light years door se, hamari galaxy ke doosre kinare ke paas ek jagah se. Wahan ek magnetar hai, yaani ek mara hua taara, jo sirf 20 kilometre chauda hai lekin hamare Suraj se bhi bhaari hai. Uski magnetic field Earth ki magnetic field se lagbhag ek quadrillion guna zyaada taakatwar hai. Kaha jaata hai ki woh Earth se Chaand ki aadhi doori par baithe hue bhi kisi credit card ka data mita sakta hai. Us raat us taare par ek bhookamp aaya. Aur ek second ke dasve hisse mein usne utni energy chhod di, jitni hamara Suraj ek lakh saal mein chhodta hai. Itni lambi doori tay karne ke baad bhi woh radiation Earth ko hila gayi, aur yahi baat scientists ko sabse zyaada chaunkaati hai. Scientists ke hisaab se agar yeh magnetar sirf 10 light years ke andar hota, to Earth ki ozone layer ka ek bada hissa ud sakta tha. Magnetar to phir bhi ek mara hua taara hai, lekin brahmand mein kuch cheezein aisi bhi hain jo poori ki poori galaxy ko peeche chhod deti hain.

CHAPTER: QUASARS
1963 mein Dutch astronomer Maarten Schmidt aasmaan ke ek chhote se neele bindu ki roshni ko padh rahe the, jo dekhne mein bilkul ek taare jaisa tha. Lekin jaise jaise unhone uski roshni ko samjha, pata chala ki woh taara tha hi nahi. Woh cheez 2 billion light years se bhi zyaada door thi, aur phir bhi aise chamak rahi thi jaise koi paas ka taara ho. Aaj aisi cheezon ko quasar kehte hain. Asal mein yeh kisi galaxy ke centre mein baitha ek bahut bada black hole hota hai, jo abhi khana kha raha hai. Uske charon taraf gas itni tezi se ghoomti hai ki ragad se lakhon degree tak garam ho jaati hai aur chamakne lagti hai. Yeh chamak itni tez hoti hai ki ek quasar apni poori galaxy ke saare taaron ki roshni ko milakar bhi hazaar guna peeche chhod deta hai. Shukar hai ki zyaadatar quasars arabon light years ki doori par hain, aur unki roshni tab nikli thi jab brahmand abhi jawaan tha. Aur inhi quasars mein se ek ke andar ab tak ke sabse bhaari black holes mein se ek chupa hua hai.

CHAPTER: TON 618
Uska naam hai TON 618, aur 1957 mein Mexico ki ek survey mein yeh bas ek halka sa neela taara lagta tha. Aaj scientists ka andaaza hai ki uske centre ke black hole ka mass hamare Suraj se 40 se 66 billion guna zyaada hai. Yeh itna bada hai ki hamare Neptune ki poori orbit uske andar aaraam se samaa jaaye, aur woh bhi ek baar nahi, bees se zyaada baar. Uski roshni ko Earth tak pahunchne mein 10 billion saal se zyaada lage hain. Yaani aaj jo TON 618 dikhta hai, woh tab ka hai jab Earth bani bhi nahi thi. Lekin ab tak ki saari jagahein kam se kam dikh to rahi thi, jabki agli jagah ko koi dekh hi nahi sakta. Aur woh jagah is pal poori Milky Way ko apni taraf kheench rahi hai.

CHAPTER: THE GREAT ATTRACTOR
Earth Suraj ke chakkar lagati hai aur Suraj galaxy ke chakkar lagata hai, yeh baat scientists bahut pehle se jaante the. Lekin 1970 aur 1980 ke beech ek ajeeb baat saamne aayi, ki Milky Way aur aas paas ki hazaaron galaxies ek hi disha mein bhaag rahi hain. Unki raftaar lagbhag 600 kilometre har second hai, itni tez ki Earth se Chaand tak ka safar bas 11 minute mein poora ho jaaye. Matlab koi bahut hi bhaari cheez in sabko apni taraf kheench rahi hai, aur scientists ne use naam diya, The Great Attractor. Mushkil bas itni hai ki us disha mein hamari apni galaxy ke taare aur dhool ek mote parde ki tarah beech mein aa jaate hain. Yeh bilkul aisa hai jaise kisi kamre mein theek aankhon ke saamne ek khambha khada ho aur uske peeche kya hai, dikhe hi nahi. Aaj itna pata hai ki us parde ke peeche galaxies ka ek bahut bada jhund hai, lekin akele woh itni kheench paida nahi kar sakta. Usse bhi aage ek aur bada jhund hai jo shayad sabko kheench raha hai, aur yeh kheenchataani abhi bhi poori tarah samjhi nahi gayi. 2014 mein scientists ne hamare is poore ilaake ka naksha banaya aur use naam diya Laniakea. Hawaii ki bhasha mein iska matlab hai, aisa aasmaan jise napa na ja sake. Brahmand mein kahin galaxies itni bheed mein hain ki ek doosre ko kheench rahi hain, aur kahin kuch hai hi nahi.

CHAPTER: THE GREAT NOTHING
1981 mein astronomer Robert Kirshner aur unki team galaxies ki doori naap rahe the, jab ek hisse mein pahunch kar unke numbers jaise ruk gaye. Wahan galaxies thi hi nahi, bas ek khaali jagah thi jo lagbhag 33 crore light years chaudi thi, aur aaj use Bootes Void kehte hain. Itni badi jagah mein hazaaron galaxies honi chahiye thi, lekin scientists ko wahan sirf lagbhag 60 galaxies mili. Astronomer Greg Aldering ne kaha tha ki agar Milky Way is void ke beech mein hoti, to 1960 tak doosri galaxies ka pata hi nahi chalta. Raat ka aasmaan apni galaxy ke taaron ke baad bilkul kaala hota, isiliye ise The Great Nothing bhi kaha jaata hai. Scientists ke hisaab se yeh shayad chhote chhote voids ke aapas mein milne se bana hai, to isme koi rahasya nahi, bas khaali jagah hai. Lekin 2015 mein isse kahin badi ek khaali jagah mili, aur shayad usne brahmand ki sabse purani roshni par bhi apna daag chhod diya tha.

CHAPTER: THE COLD SPOT
Big Bang ke lagbhag 3,80,000 saal baad brahmand itna thanda hua ki roshni pehli baar azaad hokar safar kar saki. Woh roshni aaj bhi har taraf se microwaves ke roop mein aa rahi hai, aur uska taapman lagbhag har jagah ek jaisa hai. Lekin 2004 mein aasmaan ka ek aisa hissa mila jo baaki se itna thanda tha ki use ittefaq maanna mushkil ho gaya. Uska naam pada, the Cold Spot. 2015 mein ek team ko theek usi disha mein ek supervoid mila, jo lagbhag 1.8 billion light years chauda hai, aur laga ki jawaab mil gaya. Lekin jab hisaab lagaya gaya to woh baitha nahi, kyunki yeh void akele poori thandak ko samjha hi nahi paaya. Aur yahan se kahaani thodi ajeeb ho jaati hai, kyunki kuch scientists ne ek bilkul jungli idea saamne rakha. Unke hisaab se yeh daag shayad kisi doosre brahmand se takkar ka nishaan hai, jaise saabun ka ek bulbula doosre bulbule se takra jaaye. Zyaadatar scientists is idea ko nahi maante, lekin sach yeh hai ki Cold Spot ka poora jawaab aaj tak kisi ke paas nahi hai. Aur doosre brahmand ki baat yahin khatam nahi hoti, kyunki 2008 mein kuch scientists ko laga ki unhone uska asar naap liya hai.

CHAPTER: DARK FLOW
Astronomer Alexander Kashlinsky aur unki team ne galaxies ke bade jhundon ki chaal naapi, aur unhe laga ki sainkdon jhund ek hi disha mein beh rahe hain. Yeh bilkul waisa tha jaise nadi mein bahut saare patte ek hi taraf behte chale jaayein, aur unhone ise naam diya Dark Flow. Unka idea yeh tha ki yeh kheench observable universe ke bahar se aa rahi hai, kisi aisi cheez se jise insaan kabhi dekh hi nahi sakta. Yeh khabar duniya bhar mein chhaa gayi, lekin phir 2013 mein Planck satellite ne kahin behtar data ke saath wahi jaanch dobara ki. Aur is baar Dark Flow ka koi saaf nishaan nahi mila. Kashlinsky ki team aaj bhi apne nateeje par khadi hai, jabki zyaadatar scientists maante hain ki shayad yeh flow kabhi tha hi nahi. Sach kahein to yeh baat bhi kam daravni nahi hai. Iska matlab hai ki brahmand ke kinare par kya hota hai, uska insaan bas andaaza hi laga sakta hai. Aur woh kinara asal mein hai kahan?

ENDING
Brahmand lagbhag 13.8 billion saal purana hai, lekin uska jo hissa insaan dekh sakta hai, uska kinara har disha mein lagbhag 46 billion light years door hai. Yeh doori itni zyaada isliye hai kyunki jab roshni hamari taraf safar kar rahi thi, tab space khud bhi phail raha tha. Us kinare ke aage kya hai, yeh koi nahi jaanta, shayad aur bhi galaxies hon aur shayad kuch aisa jo bilkul alag ho. Aur sabse ajeeb baat, yeh nazara dheere dheere chhota hota ja raha hai, kyunki space ke phailne ki raftaar lagaatar badh rahi hai. Door ki galaxies ek ek karke nazar se gaayab hoti jaayengi. Scientists ke hisaab se lagbhag 100 billion saal baad aasmaan mein sirf apni galaxy hi bachegi. Agar tab hamari galaxy mein koi astronomer hoga, to use kabhi pata nahi chalega ki Big Bang hua tha ya brahmand mein arabon galaxies thi. Shayad brahmand ki sabse daravni jagah koi void ya black hole nahi hai, balki woh andhera hai jo dheere dheere sab kuch dhak raha hai. Aur shayad insaan bilkul sahi time par paida hua hai, jab aasmaan mein abhi bhi dekhne ko itna kuch baaki hai.
