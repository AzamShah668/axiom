OPENING
Hamari galaxy mein lagbhag 10 crore black holes ho sakte hain. Aur unme se zyaadatar ko aaj tak kisi ne dekha nahi. Woh chamakte nahi. Unki koi roshni nahi hoti. Woh bas andhere mein chupchaap ghoomte rehte hain. 2022 mein pehli baar scientists ne aise hi ek akele black hole ko pakda. Earth se sirf 5,000 light years door. Aur yeh brahmand ki sabse daravni jagahon mein se bas pehli jagah hai. Aakhri jagah tak pahunchte pahunchte sawaal yeh nahi rahega ki brahmand kitna bada hai. Sawaal yeh hoga ki insaan kitna kam dekh sakta hai.

CHAPTER: THE INVISIBLE HUNTER
Black hole ko dekhna lagbhag namumkin hai. Woh roshni chhodta nahi, roshni ko bhi nigal jaata hai. To scientists ne use pakda kaise? Ek chaal se. 2011 mein door ka ek taara achanak zyaada chamakne laga. Lagbhag 270 din tak. Phir dheere dheere wapas normal ho gaya. Kisi cheez ne uske saamne se guzarte hue uski roshni ko mod diya tha. Bilkul waise jaise paani se bhara glass peeche rakhi cheez ko bada aur tedha dikhata hai. Hubble telescope ne agle chhe saal tak us jagah par nazar rakhi. Aur hisaab saaf tha. Wahan ek cheez thi jo hamare Suraj se kai guna bhaari thi, lekin poori tarah andheri. Na koi taara saath mein. Na kisi galaxy ka centre. Bas ek akela black hole, jo antariksh mein bhatak raha hai. Achhi khabar yeh hai ki yeh Earth ki taraf nahi aa raha. Buri khabar yeh hai ki aise black holes ko pehle se dekh paana lagbhag namumkin hai. Agar koi kabhi solar system ke paas aaya, to pehla ishaara shayad planets ki badalti chaal hogi. Black hole kam se kam chup rehta hai. Agli jagah par khatra roshni hi hai.

CHAPTER: THE MAGNETAR
December 2004 ki ek raat. Antariksh mein ghoom rahe kai satellites ke instruments ek saath bekaar ho gaye. Unse itni tez radiation takraayi thi ki unke detectors bhar gaye. Earth ki upar wali hawa bhi kuch pal ke liye hil gayi. Aur yeh radiation aayi kahan se thi? 50,000 light years door se. Galaxy ke doosre kinare ke paas se. Wahan ek magnetar hai. Ek mara hua taara, jo bas 20 kilometre chauda hai, lekin hamare Suraj se bhi bhaari hai. Uski magnetic field Earth ki magnetic field se lagbhag ek quadrillion guna taakatwar hai. Kaha jaata hai ki Earth se Chaand ki aadhi doori par bhi woh credit card ka data mita de. Us raat us taare par ek bhookamp aaya. Aur ek second ke dasve hisse mein usne itni energy chhodi, jitni hamara Suraj ek lakh saal mein chhodta hai. Yaad rahe, itni lambi doori tay karke bhi woh Earth ko hila gaya. Scientists ke hisaab se agar yeh magnetar 10 light years ke andar hota, to Earth ki ozone layer ka bada hissa ud sakta tha. Magnetar ek mara hua taara hai. Lekin brahmand mein kuch cheezein aisi bhi hain jo poori galaxy ko peeche chhod deti hain.

CHAPTER: QUASARS
1963 mein Dutch astronomer Maarten Schmidt ek chhote se neele bindu ki roshni ko padh rahe the. Woh bilkul ek taare jaisa dikhta tha. Lekin uski roshni kuch aur keh rahi thi. Woh taara tha hi nahi. Woh 2 billion light years se bhi zyaada door tha. Yaani itni door se bhi woh aise chamak raha tha jaise koi paas ka taara ho. Aaj aisi cheezon ko quasar kehte hain. Ek galaxy ke centre mein ek bahut bada black hole, jo abhi khana kha raha hai. Gas uske charon taraf tezi se ghoomti hai. Ragad se lakhon degree tak garam hoti hai. Aur chamakti hai. Itni tez ki ek quasar apni poori galaxy ke saare taaron ko milakar bhi hazaar guna peeche chhod deta hai. Shukar hai ki zyaadatar quasars arabon light years ki doori par hain. Unki roshni tab nikli thi jab brahmand jawaan tha. Aur inhi mein se ek ke andar ab tak ke sabse bhaari black holes mein se ek chupa hai.

CHAPTER: TON 618
Uska naam hai TON 618. 1957 mein Mexico ki ek survey mein yeh bas ek halka neela taara lagta tha. Aaj scientists ke andaaze se uske centre ke black hole ka mass hamare Suraj se 40 se 66 billion guna hai. Iska size? Neptune ki orbit uske andar aaraam se samaa jaaye. Ek baar nahi, bees se zyaada baar. Aur uski roshni ko Earth tak pahunchne mein 10 billion saal se zyaada lage hain. Yaani aaj jo TON 618 dikhta hai, woh tab ka hai jab Earth bani bhi nahi thi. Lekin ab tak ki saari jagahein kam se kam dikh to rahi thi. Agli jagah ko koi dekh hi nahi sakta. Aur woh is pal poori Milky Way ko apni taraf kheench rahi hai.

CHAPTER: THE GREAT ATTRACTOR
Earth Suraj ke chakkar lagati hai. Suraj galaxy ke chakkar lagata hai. Yeh sab pata tha. Lekin 1970 aur 1980 ke beech ek ajeeb baat saamne aayi. Milky Way aur aas paas ki hazaaron galaxies ek hi disha mein bhaag rahi hain. Lagbhag 600 kilometre har second ki raftaar se. Itni tez ki Earth se Chaand tak ka safar bas 11 minute mein ho jaaye. Koi bahut bhaari cheez sabko apni taraf kheench rahi hai. Scientists ne ise naam diya The Great Attractor. Mushkil bas itni hai ki woh jis disha mein hai, wahan Milky Way khud beech mein aa jaati hai. Taaron aur dhool ka ek mota parda. Jaise kisi kamre mein theek aankhon ke saamne ek khambha khada ho. Aaj itna pata hai ki parde ke peeche galaxies ka ek bahut bada jhund hai. Lekin woh akela itni kheench paida nahi kar sakta. Usse bhi aage ek aur bada jhund hai, jo shayad sabko kheench raha hai. 2014 mein scientists ne hamare is poore ilaake ka naksha banaya aur use naam diya Laniakea. Hawaii ki bhasha mein iska matlab hai, aisa aasmaan jise napa na ja sake. Kahin galaxies itni bheed mein hain ki ek doosre ko kheench rahi hain. Aur kahin kuch hai hi nahi.

CHAPTER: THE GREAT NOTHING
1981 mein astronomer Robert Kirshner aur unki team galaxies ki doori naap rahe the. Ek hisse mein pahunch kar unke numbers ruk gaye. Wahan galaxies thi hi nahi. Ek khaali jagah, 33 crore light years chaudi. Ise kehte hain Bootes Void. Itni badi jagah mein hazaaron galaxies honi chahiye thi. Mili sirf lagbhag 60. Astronomer Greg Aldering ne ek baar kaha tha ki agar Milky Way is void ke beech mein hoti, to lagbhag 1960 tak insaan ko pata hi nahi chalta ki doosri galaxies bhi hoti hain. Raat ka aasmaan apne taaron ke baad bilkul kaala hota. Isiliye ise The Great Nothing bhi kehte hain. Scientists ke hisaab se yeh shayad chhote chhote voids ke milne se bana. Koi rahasya nahi. Bas khaali jagah. Lekin 2015 mein ek aisi khaali jagah mili jo isse kahin badi thi. Aur shayad usne brahmand ki sabse purani roshni par bhi daag chhod diya tha.

CHAPTER: THE COLD SPOT
Big Bang ke lagbhag 3,80,000 saal baad brahmand itna thanda hua ki roshni pehli baar azaad hokar safar kar saki. Woh roshni aaj bhi har taraf se aa rahi hai, microwaves ke roop mein. Uska taapman lagbhag har jagah ek jaisa hai. Lekin 2004 mein aasmaan ka ek hissa mila jo baaki se thanda tha. Itna thanda ki use ittefaq maanna mushkil tha. Naam pada, the Cold Spot. 2015 mein ek team ko usi disha mein ek supervoid mila. Lagbhag 1.8 billion light years chauda. Laga ki jawaab mil gaya. Lekin hisaab baitha nahi. Yeh void poori thandak ko samjha nahi paaya. Aur yahan se kahaani ajeeb ho jaati hai. Kuch scientists ne ek jungli idea diya. Shayad yeh daag kisi doosre brahmand se takkar ka nishaan hai. Jaise saabun ka ek bulbula doosre bulbule se takra jaaye. Zyaadatar scientists is idea ko nahi maante. Lekin Cold Spot ka poora jawaab aaj tak kisi ke paas nahi hai. Aur doosre brahmand ki baat yahan khatam nahi hoti. 2008 mein kuch scientists ko laga ki unhone uska asar naap liya hai.

CHAPTER: DARK FLOW
Astronomer Alexander Kashlinsky aur unki team ne galaxies ke bade jhundon ki chaal naapi. Unhe laga ki sainkdon jhund ek hi disha mein beh rahe hain. Jaise nadi mein patte, ek hi taraf. Unhone ise naam diya Dark Flow. Idea yeh tha ki yeh kheench observable universe ke bahar se aa rahi hai. Kisi aisi cheez se jise insaan kabhi dekh hi nahi sakta. Yeh khabar duniya bhar mein chhaa gayi. Phir 2013 mein Planck satellite ne behtar data ke saath wahi jaanch ki. Aur Dark Flow ka koi saaf nishaan nahi mila. Kashlinsky ki team aaj bhi apne nateeje par khadi hai. Zyaadatar scientists maante hain ki shayad yeh flow tha hi nahi. Aur sach kahein to yeh bhi daravna hai. Kyunki iska matlab hai ki brahmand ke kinare par kya hota hai, uske baare mein insaan bas andaaza hi laga sakta hai. Aur woh kinara asal mein hai kahan?

ENDING
Brahmand lagbhag 13.8 billion saal purana hai. Lekin jo hissa insaan dekh sakta hai, uska kinara har disha mein lagbhag 46 billion light years door hai. Itna zyaada isliye kyunki jab roshni safar kar rahi thi, space khud phail raha tha. Kinare se aage kya hai, koi nahi jaanta. Shayad aur galaxies. Shayad kuch bilkul alag. Aur sabse ajeeb baat, yeh nazara dheere dheere chhota ho raha hai. Space ke phailne ki raftaar badh rahi hai. Door ki galaxies ek ek karke nazar se gaayab ho jaayengi. Scientists ke hisaab se lagbhag 100 billion saal baad, agar hamari galaxy mein koi astronomer hoga, to use aasmaan mein sirf apni galaxy dikhegi. Baaki sab andhera. Use kabhi pata nahi chalega ki Big Bang hua tha, ya brahmand mein arabon galaxies thi. Shayad brahmand ki sabse daravni jagah koi void ya black hole nahi hai. Shayad woh andhera hai jo dheere dheere sab kuch dhak raha hai. Aur shayad insaan bilkul sahi time par paida hua, jab aasmaan mein abhi bhi dekhne ko kuch baaki tha.
