## attractor_hook
Abhi, isi time, aap lagbhag {600|2} kilometre har second ki speed se kisi cheez ki taraf khinche chale ja rahe ho. Aur woh cheez kya hai, ye aaj tak kisi ne theek se dekha nahi.

## attractor_problem
Sirf aap nahi. Poori Earth, hamara Suraj, hamari Milky Way, aur aas paas ki hazaaron galaxies, sab ek hi direction mein beh rahi hain. Scientists ne us anjaan jagah ko naam diya, The Great Attractor.

## attractor_twist
Mushkil ye hai, ki woh jagah theek hamari apni galaxy ke peeche hai. Wahan dhool aur taaron ki deewar itni ghani hai, ki roshni aar paar ho hi nahi paati.
