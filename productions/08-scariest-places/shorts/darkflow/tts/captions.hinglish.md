## darkflow_hook
{2008|3} mein NASA ke ek scientist ne aisa daava kiya, jisne sabko hila diya. Ki hamare dikhne waale universe ke bahar se, koi cheez galaxies ko apni taraf kheench rahi hai.

## darkflow_problem
Alexander Kashlinsky aur unki team ne dekha, ki saikdon galaxy clusters ek hi direction mein beh rahe the, jaise nadi mein patte behte hain. Lekin jitna door hum dekh sakte hain, utni door tak us taraf koi itni bhaari cheez thi hi nahi.

## darkflow_twist
Unka kehna tha, ki ye khinchaav observable universe ki seema ke bahar se aa raha hai, ek aisi jagah se, jahan ki roshni kabhi hum tak pahunch hi nahi sakti. Ise naam diya gaya, Dark Flow.

## darkflow_end
Phir {2013|3} mein, Planck satellite ne kahin behtar data ke saath ise dobara check kiya. Usne kya dekha? Ye jaanne ke liye, poori video dekhiye.
