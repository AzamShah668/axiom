## Their factual slips (fix, don't copy)
| Their claim | Correct |
|---|---|
| Boötes Void found by "Robert Kishner" | Robert **Kirshner** et al., 1981 |
| Sgr A* found 1974 by "Bruce Bell and Robert Brown" | **Bruce Balick** and Robert Brown, 1974 |
| Event horizon radius by "Carl Schwarz" | **Karl Schwarzschild**, 1916 |
| First quasar 3C 273 by "Martin Schmidt" | **Maarten Schmidt**, 1963 |
| Dark flow as an established fact | Kashlinsky et al. 2008 (WMAP); Planck (2013) found no significant dark flow: **disputed** |
| Rogue black hole "NASA confirmed", "millions" | 2022, Hubble data (OGLE-2011-BLG-0462, about 5,000 ly), two teams; estimates of ~100 million in the Milky Way |
| TON 618 "66 billion suns" as settled | 66 billion is the 2004 estimate; a 2019 estimate is about 40 billion: say "lagbhag 40 se 66 billion" |
| Singularities "exist all across the universe" | predicted by general relativity; nobody knows what is really there: hedge |
| Eridanus Supervoid 1.3 billion ly away, 1 billion ly across | Szapudi et al. 2015: about 3 billion ly away, up to ~1.8 billion ly across; its link to the Cold Spot is debated |
