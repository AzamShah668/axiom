## Rules for each photo
- **Licence:** free to use on a monetised YouTube channel. That means one of:
  - public domain;
  - CC0;
  - CC BY (any version);
  - CC BY-SA (any version).

  Good sources are Wikimedia Commons, NASA, the Library of Congress, ESO and university press pages that state a CC licence.
- **Not allowed:**
  - Nobel Foundation or nobelprize.org photos;
  - news or press agencies (AP, Reuters, Getty);
  - "fair use" or "all rights reserved" images;
  - screenshots from other videos;
  - AI-generated likenesses.
- **The picture itself:**
  - a clear photo of the face, head and shoulders or upper body;
  - at least 800 px on the short side;
  - no large text, watermark or logo across it.
- **Credit for each photo:** the page link, the author or photographer, and the licence name. The video description has to credit it.

## How to send them
1. Put the photos and a `credits.txt` in one Drive folder, or in one zip file.
2. Use the file names from the table. In `credits.txt`, write one line per photo:

```
Hubble.jpg | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:... | author name | Public domain
Perlmutter.jpg | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:... | Roy Kaltschmidt (LBNL) | CC BY-SA 4.0
```

3. Share it as **"Anyone with the link can view"** and paste the link in the chat.
4. A shared link works best: my Drive access can only see files that I created myself.

If no free photo exists for someone, write "none" for that person in `credits.txt`. They'll get a typed name card instead.
