CHAPTER: THE MISTAKE THAT WASN'T
Sau saal pehle, Albert Einstein ko yakeen tha ki universe hamesha se ek jaisa hai, na badhta hai, na ghatta hai.
Unki apni equations kehti thi ki universe ko ya to phailna chahiye ya simatna, isliye unhone usme ek extra number jod diya, jise cosmological constant kehte hain.

Universe phail raha tha.

CHAPTER: THE BIG FREEZE
Ise kehte hain Big Freeze.
Is raaste mein universe hamesha phailta rahega, aur galaxies ek doosre se itni door chali jaayengi, ki unki roshni kabhi kisi tak pahunchegi hi nahi.
Sau billion saal baad, aasmaan mein sirf apni galaxy ke taare dikhenge.
Baaki sab, hamesha ke liye nazar se gaayab.
Unnees sau sattaanve mein do astronomers, Fred Adams aur Gregory Laughlin, ne universe ke is lambe bhavishya ka poora hisaab lagaya.
Unke hisaab se sau trillion saal baad, woh gas khatam ho jaayegi jisse taare bante hain.
Phir jo chhote laal taare bachenge, woh bhi ek ek karke bujh jaayenge.
Is time ko ek chhota sa hisaab samjhaata hai.
Agar Big Bang se aaj tak ke terah point aath billion saal ko ek din maan liya jaaye, to taaron ka banna bees saal baad band hoga.
Uske baad universe mein sirf thande, mare hue taare bachenge, aur black holes.
Black holes bhi hamesha nahi rahenge.
Stephen Hawking ne dikhaya tha, ki black holes bhi bahut dheere dheere radiation ke roop mein apna wazan kho dete hain.
Sabse bade black holes ko khatam hone mein, ek ke aage sau se bhi zyaada zero jitne saal lagenge.
Aur phir, kuch nahi.
Na roshni, na garmi, na koi badlaav.
Universe ek thanda, khaali aur hamesha ke liye shaant andhera ban jaayega, jise scientists heat death kehte hain.
Yeh ant dheema hai, lekin shaant hai.
Doosra raasta, iska bilkul ulta hai.
