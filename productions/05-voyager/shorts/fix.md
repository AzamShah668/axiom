**Description**

In 2023 one memory chip on Voyager 1 failed, about 3 percent of its memory, and the spacecraft 24 billion kilometres away started sending nonsense. The chip could not be repaired, so NASA's engineers moved the code stuck in it, piece by piece, to the rest of the memory and sent the fix by radio. Two days later, Voyager answered with clean data.
