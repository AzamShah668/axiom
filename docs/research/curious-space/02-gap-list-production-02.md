## What we already do better
- **Real pictures.** 4K archive footage of real objects, every shot different. They reuse the same AI renders
  (the spacecraft side view at 0:14, 1:18, 2:26, 7:52), and much of the space is invented. That is a
  credibility and monetisation risk for them (YouTube's 2025 policy on mass-produced and repetitive AI
  content).
- **Facts and credits.** A fact-check table and full footage and music credits. They have typos on screen
  (`FEBURURY`), mixed digit grouping, and a disclaimer instead of credits.
- **Loudness.** -14.1 LUFS. They master at -19.9, so YouTube plays them about 6 dB quieter than other videos.
- **Navigable structure.** Ten countdown chapters with YouTube chapters.
- **Tone.** Third-person documentary. Theirs depends on "hum" and "aap" and on India-specific comparisons that
  don't travel.

## What neither does (to get ahead)
1. **Syllable-exact motion graphics.** ElevenLabs gives us character timings, so every kinetic word, count-up
   landing, riser end and cut can be placed on the exact syllable, automatically and in every video. They time
   by hand.
2. **Real-data graphics.** Charts and diagrams from published data (the GW150914 chirp, the S2 orbit, GOES flux,
   EHT images): an authenticity AI art can't fake.
3. **A data HUD per video.** A slim readout that updates fact by fact (e.g. mass, size, distance of each black
   hole). A journey dashboard built from real numbers.
4. **Clean picture plus a full English caption track.** Burned-in text only for meaning, and a complete English
   CC track uploaded with the video (we already write a caption for every sentence). It's accessible, and a
   non-Hindi audience gets the whole story.
5. **A recap at the end.** A 15 s montage of the ten numbers before the close, for re-watch and comment value.

## Decisions for you
1. Captions: replace the running subtitle with kinetic key phrases + an uploaded English CC track (recommended),
   or keep a lighter subtitle line under the new graphics.
2. Music: raise the bed to 16-18 LU under the voice (from 26), and drop the pumping.
3. Pauses: allow 3-5 planned 1.5-3 s breaths in the narration (rendered with the locked voice, as gaps between
   sections or "…" pauses).
4. Logo bug top right: yes or no.
5. Thumbnail and title test as in gap 10: yes or no.
