# Curious Space (@curiousstudiosofficial): reverse-engineering notes

A Hindi space channel that started in July 2026. Nine uploads by October 2026: two "What If" episodes
broke out (604K and 555K views on a 2.9K-subscriber channel), the other series ("Next 100 Years In Space",
"Cosmic Anomalies") sit at 2K-44K. The format that works is a hypothetical journey told as a ride: AI-made
cinematic shots of an invented spacecraft, cut with dense kinetic typography and animated data.

## Breakdowns
| # | Video | Views | Key takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [What If We Land On Sun?](01-what-if-we-land-on-sun.md) | 555K in 12 days | A 68 s hook of six "impossible numbers", then a layer-by-layer ride to the core with a persistent journey frame (vehicle, distance and temperature counters, stage footer). Every number is an animated card. Music is a flat bed 15 LU under the voice; the sound design lives in five deliberate pauses. |
