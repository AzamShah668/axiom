Watch-outs:
- "teaspoon = Mount Everest" is about 100× too high; use "about a billion tonnes".
- The 2004 flare distance is ~28,000 ly (2008 revision), not 50,000; attribute the Sun comparison to NASA.
- Gold estimates for GW170817 vary widely; say "one estimate".
- NANOGrav is "evidence", not a detection.
- Substitute if pulsar-planet footage is too thin: Webb finds the neutron star in SN 1987A (2024).
