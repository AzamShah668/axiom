## What the curves say (pattern in 6 of 6 videos)
1. **Most viewers are lost in the first 60-90 s** (35-43 points), mostly right after the hook ends, at 0:30-0:45.
   The drop lines are slow context: a date and a place ("In China, 1054...", "In 2011 Swift detected..."), a definition
   ("If all of an object's mass is squeezed..."), or a meta line ("By the last one, the question won't be...").
2. **Chapter 1 loses another 10-26 points** where it opens with history (names, years, letters) before anything happens.
3. **After ~3 minutes the curve is nearly flat**: whoever survives the first minutes usually stays. The ending is not the problem.
4. **Shorter videos keep a larger share**: 04 and 05 (10.5-10.7 min) keep 42-45% on average and 28% at the end;
   02 and 07 (13-14 min) keep 24-32% and 18-21%.
5. **Spikes (rewatches) come on concrete, vivid moments**: a named person with a photo, a dramatic number, a new place.
6. 62% of 08's views come from suggested videos: viewers who did not search for the topic decide in seconds.

## Fixes (to the script skill, script_check and the edit planner)
- Opening <= 30 s: the most dramatic fact in the first sentence, the promise of the payoff, and a first mini-payoff by 0:20.
- No date, place-name or definition in the first 60 s; history starts only after the viewer is hooked.
- Chapter 1 = the strongest story, opening on an event, not on background.
- A re-hook (open question) every 20-30 s for the first 3 minutes, then every 45-60 s.
- Videos of 7-9 minutes (about 1,000-1,200 words).
- Visual change at least every 2.5 s in the first minute; a photo, number card or new place on every name and number.
