## How their first 30 seconds work (in our words)
1. **The first sentence is the whole stakes, in under ten words.** "The universe will die one day." No date, no
   name, no setup.
2. **Then 3-4 very short questions in a row** (why, how, forever?, how do we know?). The questions are the
   promise of the video. *Our rule bans narrator questions; that is a big part of why our openings feel flat.*
3. **Escalation:** a scary superlative, a one-line reaction ("as if that weren't enough"), then something
   bigger ("they might delete the universe itself").
4. **Straight into the content by 10 s,** with an everyday picture by 30 s (a ball thrown up that never comes
   down = accelerating expansion).
5. **A human, casual voice:** short, uneven sentences, a little humour, talking to "you"
   ("what happens if you fall into one?").

## Why our scripts read like AI (owner, 2026-11-15)
Every sentence the same length; "Lekin" and hedges in every other line; explaining before intriguing; no
reaction lines; lists read out in order; openings that set up instead of hitting.
