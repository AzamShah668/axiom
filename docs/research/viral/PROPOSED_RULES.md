# Proposed script rules from the viral research (DRAFT, for the owner to accept or change)

The owner is still writing the script rules; nothing here is in force until the owner says so.
Sources: `hindi_longform.md`, `english_longform.md`, `shorts.md` (this folder) and our own retention study (`docs/research/retention/`).
Limits: YouTube pages were blocked from the sandbox, so titles and view counts are real, but few spoken hooks are.

## What our own numbers say (strongest evidence: it is our audience)
- 35-43% of viewers leave in the first 60-90 s, right after the hook, when background starts. Chapter 1 loses another 10-26%. After ~3 min the curve is flat.
- The 10.5-minute video held better than the longer ones. 62% of views come from suggested videos.
- So the opening 90 s matter most, and they are where background/explanation must be cut hardest.

## Long video: proposed rules
1. **Sell one strange thing, not a topic.** First sentence states the anomaly or a too-big number; no "aaj hum baat karenge". (Hindi Tables A-B: "Something STRANGE…" titles 5-8M; English rule 1.)
2. **Promise with a number inside 30 s**, and say what the viewer will know by the end. (English rule 2.)
3. **Delay background until a question needs it.** Background answers a question the viewer already has; never a history block straight after the hook. (Our retention drop at 60-90 s.)
4. **3-5 open loops in the first two minutes, closed in order; one big loop kept open to ~75-85%.** (English rule 3.)
5. **Make the viewer guess a plausible wrong answer, then correct it.** "Aapko kya lagta hai…?" (English rule 4.)
6. **Every 1-3 minute block has a "lekin" turn**; a block that only adds information is cut or merged. (English rule 6.)
7. **One everyday Indian analogy per concept** (cricket ball, Mumbai local, rupees, Himalaya); one Indian anchor per video, not in every title. (Hindi rule 7, English rule 9.)
8. **Viewer inside the story**: "aap/socho" as the person falling, measuring, waiting; two scales of stakes (personal + cosmic). (English rule 8.)
9. **End by echoing the opening** and handing off one specific unanswered question (the next video). (English rule 10.)
10. **Title: 4-9 words, Roman script, one emotional word** (Strange, Impossible, Wrong) or a yes/no question the video answers "not exactly". (Hindi rules 1-3.)

**Open decision for the owner (length):** the big Hindi channels' hits run 18-33 min, but our own retention says shorter holds better, and the owner said "I think we should make shorter videos". Suggest 10-13 min until our retention after 3 min is proven, then test one 18-minute video.

## Shorts: proposed rules
1. Hook in the first breath (under 2 s): the most extreme fact or an absurd what-if, never the topic name.
2. State the surprise, hold back the "why"; one fact per sentence; English only for names and numbers.
3. "Socho/tum" once in the first 5 s.
4. Three beats (hook, two "but" steps, payoff), payoff by ~80% of the runtime, then stop.
5. A new visual every 1.5-2.5 s (the web3d scenes do this); captions always on, PLAIN text.
6. End on a line that loops back to the hook, or the 1-second "full video" card; no like/subscribe.
The owner's three 09 Shorts already follow 1, 3 and 4 ("Socho…" openings, a turn, then the question for the full video).
