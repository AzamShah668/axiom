## 3. Patterns

### Title formulas [V from Tables A-C]
1. "Something [adj] is happening / found [place]" : "Something STRANGE Found in Deep Sea", "Something is Happening on Mars", "Something Strange Is Happening at the Bottom of the Ocean", "Something Weird is Happening Inside Earth" (Dhruv, 14.7M), "Something Incredible Was Hidden Beneath Antarctica" (456K), "NASA Found Something Impossible on Mars" (327K). The vague noun "something" is the single most repeated word in the top videos.
2. "Scientists found / just proved X" (Gaurav 1.0M, Antariksh 420-670K, Rahasya "वैज्ञानिकों ने...").
3. A named mystery in "The X Mystery" or a plain question: "Did Scientists Finally Prove The Existence of Soul?" (5.1M), "Did We Found a Real Wormhole?" (860K), "Can't ..." questions.
4. Scale/stakes: "How small is Earth compared to the entire Universe?", "Reaching Another Star May Be Impossible!", "Will China Control the Moon?"
5. Contrarian: "We Were Wrong About Super El Nino / Megalodon", "Aliens CONFIRMED?".
6. Indian angle: "India's Area 51 is Real?", "India's Internet has a Dangerous Deep Sea Weak Spot", "Nepal's Flood". Mid-performers (700K-820K), not the top.
- Title length: top titles are 4-9 words, English or Hinglish-in-Roman. Devanagari-heavy long titles (Rahasya) correlate with low views in this sample [V correlation, not proven causation; also a much smaller audience].
- Topic: biology/brain/deep sea/mystery beats pure astrophysics on Gaurav's channel: only one black-hole long video in 48, 1.0M, vs soul/ocean/Mars 5-8M [V].

### Length [V]
- Hit videos 18-33 min (Gaurav 18:44-32:09; Dhruv 22-29 min; Antariksh 15-22 min).
- Under 12 min performs poorly in the Rahasya and Secrets-of-the-Universe-style samples for short explainers [V weakly]. 1h+ interviews underperform [V].
- Our target for a first viral attempt: 15-22 min [E]. At 54 subs, retention beats length.

### Hooks, pace, language [E, no transcripts]
- Likely hook types in the first 10 s: an unexplained anomaly stated flatly ("scientists found X and cannot explain it"), a direct question to the viewer, or a number that is too big. Based on the title patterns above; the actual spoken hooks are unverified.
- "Aap" address and Indian comparisons (cricket pitch, Mumbai local, rupees, Himalayas, ISRO): common in this genre [E]; the Indian-angle titles in Table A/C are verified, the narration use is not.
- Chapter structure, endings, thumbnail text: not verified. [E] Thumbnails of these channels use 1-3 word English caps text with a red circle/arrow on one strange object; Gaurav's "STRANGE" in caps appears in the title itself [V title].

## 4. Ten rules (each tied to evidence)

1. **Sell one unexplained thing, not a topic.** "Something STRANGE Found in Deep Sea" 8.1M vs plain topic titles. Evidence: Tables A, B. Write the title as "Something [adjective] [place/object]" or "Scientists found X" and make the script answer exactly that.
2. **Keep the title at 4-9 words, Roman script, one emotional adjective** (STRANGE, Impossible, Weird, Wrong). Evidence: all top titles; Rahasya's long Devanagari+English titles sit at 2K-20K.
3. **Make the question the viewer's, not the subject's.** "Did we find a real wormhole?" 860K; "Did scientists prove the soul exists?" 5.1M; "Reaching another star may be impossible!" 725K. Use a yes/no question with a hedged answer so the video earns a "not exactly".
4. **Target 18-25 minutes for a first hit, not an hour.** Evidence: Gaurav's 1h+ videos 116K-816K vs 5-8M at 27-32 min; Antariksh's 15-22 min all 160K-860K. Over 30 min only with a mystery that sustains it.
5. **Pick the topic by proven demand: deep sea, Mars, brain/consciousness, "wrong about X", Antarctica/Moon/hidden-place mysteries, wormholes, black holes with a twist** (Antariksh: planet near black hole 420K; Gaurav: black hole bomb 1.0M). Plain "what is a black hole" is the crowded format with no verified hits here.
6. **Voyager / edge-of-universe / alien-contact questions are the proven small-channel hits** (Rahasya: Voyager Oort Cloud 197K, "edge of the universe" 99K, aliens find Voyager 76K, vs 2K-5K for generic NASA update titles). Good fit for our Hindi documentary format.
7. **Add one Indian anchor per video** (ISRO, Himalaya, Indian ocean cable, Alaknanda galaxy) but do not put it in every title: Indian-angle titles score mid-pack (300K-820K), the universal-mystery ones score highest. [V titles; narration use E]
8. **Pay off the title early and keep escalating.** [E] Say the strange fact in the first sentence, give the "why it matters" within 30 s, then use chapters each ending on a new open question. Gaurav's own recent 1.58M-in-6-days "Human Brain While It Died" title promises the answer in the title itself.
9. **Refresh the same story with a new "something" every 1-2 weeks.** Gaurav's Mars title is reused (Oct 2026 short 1.86M, Sep 2026 long 7.15M): the same title format works as long and short. Cut a Short from every long video.
10. **Test with a copy-paste loop before shipping.** Because transcripts were not retrievable here, collect 5 real openings from the top videos above and compare our cold open to them: first sentence states the anomaly, no greeting/logo preamble, no more than 2 sentences before the first number or stake. [E]
